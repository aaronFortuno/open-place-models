# Basílica de la Sagrada Família · r1

Barcelona, Spain. This print model represents the main exterior volumes of the basilica in early 2026, after the upper arm of the cross was installed on 20 February. It includes eight historic bell towers, six central lanterns, the Nativity façade relief and the western sacristy dome. The scope ends at the built lower massing of the Glory façade. [Completion of the cross](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/-/una-creu-que-arriba-al-cel-de-barcelona) · [Architecture booklet](https://sagradafamilia.org/documents/20142/1693653/SF_Quadern_04_20260227_digital_AF.pdf/f426e99d-cde5-b8cb-350b-ea7fc650b672)

## Survey and reconstruction

- **Municipal CAD.** The base, plan and stepped roof levels use 92 objects from the Ajuntament de Barcelona's Sagrada Família neighbourhood DWG, released on 20 May 2026. The archive describes flat extruded multipatches and gives their ground and roof elevations. We kept the main connected ground outline, simplified its edges and combined adjacent parts. The urban model supplies the overall massing of the nave, transepts, apse and built lower portion of the Glory façade. The CAD release date describes the archive, whose capture date is unspecified. Fine window tracery, sculpture, interior details and roof geometry beyond the documented tiers are omitted. [Municipal CAD product and licence](https://portaldades.ajuntament.barcelona.cat/mapes/146) · [Sagrada Família neighbourhood DWG](https://w20.bcn.cat/CartoBCN/getFile.ashx?prod=146.06.47)
- **Measured historic profiles.** We cut the ICGC triangle survey from the 19 December 2014 oblique aerial flight at 14–16 heights per bell tower, and measured radii along up to 48 horizontal rays per height. The model smooths the incomplete lower sections, preserves the taper and upper widening, and closes each pinnacle with a small printable cap. The measured tips are about 104, 114, 114 and 104 m above the common model ground at the Passion façade, and 100.5, 109, 109 and 100.4 m at the Nativity façade. These are photogrammetric estimates relative to the model ground. The relief above the Nativity central portal follows the scanned frontal envelope, with the cypress and its finial reaching about 65.6 m. Thin sculptural details are absorbed into that envelope. [ICGC triangle survey](https://betaportal.icgc.cat/model-3d-de-la-sagrada-familia-en-alta-resolucio/) · [Flight date](https://betaserver.icgc.cat/potree12/examples/barcelonasagradafamilia.html) · [Nativity façade booklet](https://sagradafamilia.org/documents/20142/1693653/SF_Quadern_06_20260227_digital_AF.pdf/379fa0e6-018d-194b-b389-74ad8085ad18)
- **Dimensioned reconstruction.** The six central towers were completed after the 2014 flight. Their positions use the CAD and their outer profiles interpolate the operator's published dimensions. The Jesus tower has 12 facets, begins its stone-panel stage at 85 m, reaches 142.5 m at the last panel and ends at 172.5 m. The cross is 17 m high with a 13.5 m arm span, aligned to the temple. The Mary tower reaches 138 m with a faceted 7.5 m twelve-pointed star. The four Evangelist towers reach 135 m with simplified wing envelopes and bridges at 85 m. The western sacristy's faceted dome and terminal reach 43 m. Intermediate radii, bridge widths, terminal profiles and transitions are approximations between the documented points. The individual Evangelist figures and the future Glory bell towers are omitted. [Bell towers, lanterns and sacristies booklet](https://sagradafamilia.org/documents/20142/1693653/SF_Quadern_10_20260227_digital_AF.pdf/0e3991bd-120b-8aa4-56e8-4f1cbe9e29d6) · [Cross dimensions and arms](https://sagradafamilia.org/es/-/instal-lats-els-quatre-bracos-horitzontals-de-la-creu-de-la-torre-de-jesucrist) · [Evangelist tower levels](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/-/s-ornamenta-amb-trencadis-l-icosaedre-de-l-evangelista-mateu) · [Evangelist wings](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/-/la-sagrada-familia-comenca-a-col-locar-les-peces-que-culminaran-les-torres-dels-evangelistes-lluc-i-marc) · [Mary tower star](https://sagradafamilia.org/detalle/-/asset_publisher/7JWibz9G2KUY/content/l-estel-ja-corona-la-torre-de-la-mare-de-deu)

## Registration, provenance and reuse

The anchor is `[2.17436, 41.40363]`, corresponding to `[430987.434333, 4583894.262430]` in ETRS89 / UTM 31N. The model uses a common ground plane at 32 m in the CAD. Its axes are X east, Y up and Z south, in metres. The catalogue footprint is the main outer ring of OpenStreetMap relation `9194723` version 33. Eight small exterior rings from the same relation are outside the model's coverage. [OpenStreetMap relation](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/9194723)

The `survey-layout.json` fixture records the municipal archive and DWG checksums, retained object handles, CAD heights and local outlines, plus the 74 ICGC tile paths, section samples and coordinate conversion. The CAD subset came from the top faces of extruded meshes in a LibreDWG 0.14 conversion. Candidate polygons overlapped the mapped basilica by at least 65% and were filtered to the connected building. The ICGC tiles are listed in the CZML, with the local triangle coordinates transformed using each tile's georeferenced origin. To regenerate the GLB from the included measurements and reconstruction parameters, install the pinned dependencies and run `node scripts/generate-sagrada-familia.mjs` from the repository root. ## Validation

- The GLB is 119,204 bytes and contains 4,917 vertices and 9,854 triangles in a single closed component. Its extent is 117.95 × 93.21 m in plan and 172.5 m high, with a signed volume of 300,902 m³. The audit found zero boundary edges, orientation conflicts, degenerate triangles, disconnected incident face rings and duplicate positions. The horizontal cuts at 115 and 130 m show six separate central lanterns, the 136 m cut shows the two tallest towers, and the central cross section is one continuous outline.
- Local Relleu Obert checks used ICGC and OSM at 1:1,500, Catastre and OSM3D at 1:3,000 and 27°, and a partial ICGC crop at 1:1,500 and 9°. They confirmed terrain contact, preservation of neighbouring geometry and the eight exterior OSM fragments, and the building-height limit and hidden-building controls. The audited ICGC 3MF measured 248,020 bytes with 20,538 triangles. The local catalogue build and viewer checks covered search, size, rotation, zoom and the GLB link.
- The model stands 115 mm high at 1:1,500 and 57.5 mm at 1:3,000, before the terrain. The 4.5 m cross arms become 3 mm and 1.5 mm thick respectively. The smallest pinnacle caps may be finer than a standard extrusion line at either scale. Physical slicing, supports and printing remain to be checked.
