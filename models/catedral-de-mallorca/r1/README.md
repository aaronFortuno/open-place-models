# Catedral de Mallorca · r1

La Seu in Palma has a long, low-pitched central roof, a stepped east end, a double row of flying buttresses on each side, a square north bell tower and the four pinnacled towers of the west front. This revision models that exterior, including the attached Almoina and cloister roofs and their open court. The Almudaina, Diocesan Museum and retaining walls are separate. The geometry leaves out interior fittings and artworks. [Cathedral history](https://catedraldemallorca.org/catedral/historia/) · [Bell tower and cloister conservation report](https://www.cultura.gob.es/planes-nacionales/dam/jcr%3A0aaa0ddb-564b-4a41-9070-77c289f6dd2e/catedral-palma-mallorca.pdf)

## Measurements and reconstruction

- **Plan and roofs.** The PNOA orthophoto fixes the plan direction, about 30.44° south of UTM grid east from the main portal to the apse. Readable PNOA surface pixels put the main roof near 61–63 m, the aisle roofs near 47–50 m and the Royal Chapel roof near 45–49 m orthometric. The mesh uses a shallow ridge at 63.7 m, two aisles at about 48 m, and separate, lower terraces and the stepped Royal and Trinity chapels. The surfaces are fitted manually to the orthophoto and valid elevation returns. The cathedral's published heights of 43.74 m, 30.17 m and 16.28 m refer to the central nave, aisle and side chapel interiors respectively. [PNOA elevation services](https://pnoa.ign.es/pnoa-lidar/visualizadores-y-servicios-web) · [PNOA orthophotos](https://pnoa.ign.es/pnoa-imagen/visualizadores-y-servicios-web) · [Cathedral measurements](https://blog.catedraldemallorca.org/opinion/joan-bauza/tomando-medidas/)
- **Flying buttresses.** The eight bay lines follow the orthophoto and the ground plan in Fuentes, Figure 8. Both tiers are open above the side aisles. Figure 9 gives a typical span of 9.22 m and a soffit radius of 8.8 m. The single simplified section is repeated at each bay, with the height difference and the masonry piers informed by Figure 7. The model uses 1.74 m-thick polygonal flyers and simplified pinnacles. It does not reproduce individual arch deformation or stonework. [Fuentes 2018, Figs. 7–9](https://msp.org/jomms/2018/13-5/jomms-v13-n5-p03-s.pdf)
- **West front, campanile and rose windows.** The west gable, tall inner turrets and lower outer turrets follow the cathedral's photographs of the Peyronnet façade. The west crown and bell tower top use the published heights of roughly 62 m and 52 m over a 16.6 m floor reference. Narrow crests are unresolved in the 5 m surface. The square bell tower is slightly off the nave axis and its cornices and upper openings are simplified. The eastern rose opening is 11.31 m across as published. Its position and the smaller west rose are approximated from the surveyed section and façade photographs. Roses, portals and lancets use shallow blind recesses so they remain legible while keeping the shell solid. Glass, tracery, statuary and carved details are omitted. [Cathedral measurements](https://blog.catedraldemallorca.org/opinion/joan-bauza/tomando-medidas/) · [Cathedral west front](https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/fachada-principal-juan-bautista-peyronnet/bQGH_toamEb9yw?hl=es) · [Conservation report](https://www.cultura.gob.es/planes-nacionales/dam/jcr%3A0aaa0ddb-564b-4a41-9070-77c289f6dd2e/catedral-palma-mallorca.pdf)
- **Foundation.** The terrain near the west entrance and north annex lies roughly at 16–17 m. It falls toward about 12–15 m along the southeast edge. The flat underside at 13.2 m is a modelling datum for embedding the building and preserving the absolute height of the roof. The footprint comes from OSM way `25883629`, version 55 on 8 November 2026. The opening of the cloister courtyard comes from the orthophoto. [OSM footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/25883629) · [PNOA elevation services](https://pnoa.ign.es/pnoa-lidar/visualizadores-y-servicios-web)

## Registration, source and reuse

The WGS84 anchor is `[2.64831858276, 39.56747478576]`. The GLB uses metres with X east, Y up and Z south, and spans 117.43 × 96.25 m in plan. Y=0 is at 13.2 m orthometric on the PNOA elevation reference. The highest west pinnacle is 65.4 m above that datum. Relleu Obert v0.23.36 or later can use `anchorElevation` to keep that placement while fitting the building to its terrain.

The revision uses sampled PNOA 5 m terrain, 5 m absolute surface and 2.5 m normalised building surface data, plus PNOA maximum-actuality orthophotography. The reduced fixture records the captured rasters and the working estimates. Architectural publications and photographs serve as references for an original simplified solid. With the pinned catalogue dependencies installed, run `node scripts/generate-catedral-de-mallorca.mjs`. An optional path writes the matching GeoJSON feature. The GLB regenerated to SHA-256 `66ea6439c9f847beee58a3c047ec6364065091ad2684f299d3be8910084ae8bb`.

## Validation

- The default GLB is 52,872 bytes, with 2,133 vertices and 4,406 triangles. An independent audit found one watertight solid, consistent face orientation, positive signed volume of 234,380.52 m³ and no exact duplicate positions, degenerate triangles or disconnected face rings. The double open flying arches, west braces and court account for the greater size relative to the usual catalogue target.
- Relleu Obert tested the full model with OpenStreetMap at 1:2,000 and with OpenFreeMap at 1:4,500, rotated 26°. The OSM cathedral way and the corresponding OpenFreeMap fragment were replaced. The Almudaina and Diocesan Museum were retained. A partial OSM crop at 1:2,500 and −19°, centred 72 m farther east, produced closed geometry. Height limiting and building hiding also passed. In the 1:2,000 selection a longitudinal ray below the south flyers at elevation 47.7 m passed through the opening, while at 51.3 m it intersected 14 mesh faces. The west crown reached 78.6 m.
- At 1:2,000 the solid is about 58.7 × 48.1 × 32.7 mm. The 1.74 m flyers scale to 0.87 mm thick at 1:2,000 and 0.58 mm at 1:3,000. At 1:4,500 they are about 0.39 mm, where the slicer may omit them with a 0.4 mm nozzle. Slicing and physical printing remain to be checked.
