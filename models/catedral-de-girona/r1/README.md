# Catedral de Girona · r1

The exterior model covers the Gothic church and its chevet, the Baroque west front and bell tower, the Romanesque Torre de Carlemany and the cloister with its open courtyard. The cathedral square and monumental stair are outside the building footprint. The terrain source supplies the stair's slope and landings where its resolution allows. [Cathedral](https://catedraldegirona.cat/obres/la-catedral-de-girona/) · [West façade and stair](https://www.guiavisualcatedralgirona.cat/la-facana-principal/) · [Cloister](https://www.guiavisualcatedralgirona.cat/el-claustre/)

## Measurements and reconstruction

- **Nave and chevet.** The ICGC LiDAR surface at 1 m and cross-sections through the 2014 oblique aerial point cloud put the nave eaves at about 132.5 m and the ridge at 136.6 m. The eastern choir has a lower, nearly flat roof at about 122.5 m. The ambulatory and radial chapels step down from it. The broad roof planes and walls follow these sections. Small pinnacles and the roof-height forms of the flying buttresses are simplified, with a solid web in place of the open undersides. [ICGC elevation products](https://www.icgc.cat/ca/Geoinformacio-i-mapes/Dades-i-productes/Elevacions/Elevacions-territorial/Models-delevacions) · [ICGC oblique survey](https://betaportal.icgc.cat/model-3d-de-girona/) · [Chevet architecture](https://www.guiavisualcatedralgirona.cat/la-capcalera-de-la-catedral/)
- **Baroque west front.** An orthogonal projection through the oblique survey locates the portal, the main façade bands, the rose and the row of seven openings above it. The rose opening is about 6.9 m wide in the model. These features are shallow relief and recesses for small-scale printing. Tracery and statues are omitted. The architectural study and visual guide confirm their arrangement. [West front](https://www.guiavisualcatedralgirona.cat/la-facana-principal/) · [Architectural survey and history](https://revistes.uab.cat/locus/article/download/188/188-pdf-ca)
- **Towers.** Horizontal slices of the point cloud set the profiles of the main tower's broad lower shaft, irregular octagonal belfry and dome. The west landing is about 97 m in the registered point cloud. The local LiDAR maximum is 165.031 m, compared with the 67 m overall height cited by the cathedral guide. The uppermost mesh point adopts the LiDAR elevation. A thickened, symbolic finial takes the place of the narrow weathervane sculpture. The belfry arches are shallow recesses. The shorter Torre de Carlemany beside the cloister is about 134.6 m at its parapet, and its upper paired windows are simplified as recesses. [Main bell tower](https://www.guiavisualcatedralgirona.cat/la-facana-principal/) · [Torre de Carlemany](https://www.guiavisualcatedralgirona.cat/la-torre-de-carlemany/)
- **Cloister and foundations.** The OSM outline defines the trapezoidal open court at about 398 m². A local adjustment at the southeast corner increases the opening to about 401 m² and retains the mapped basin, including the small displacement introduced by vector tile generalisation. The low roofs and south annexes are interpolated from local surface samples, using the orthophoto to distinguish their layout. The 0.5 m LiDAR terrain sets the underside with about 2.7 m of initial embedment. Lower walls visible in the standalone mesh are intended to fit into the surrounding hillside. The cloister arcades and the 90 individual stair treads are omitted. [ICGC local orthophoto](https://www.icgc.cat/ca/Geoinformacio-i-mapes/Dades-i-productes/Imatge/Ortofoto-local) · [OSM footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/1012115) · [OSM basin](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/65666817) · [Stair and landings](https://www.guiavisualcatedralgirona.cat/la-facana-principal/)

## Registration, source and reuse

The WGS84 anchor is `[2.8263392627, 41.9874374113]`. The baked GLB is in metres with X east, Y up and Z south. Y=0 is at 80.724 m orthometric height above the Alicante datum. Relleu Obert v0.23.36 and later can use the separate `anchorElevation` value to preserve the slope and place the roof at its observed elevation. The mesh spans about 88.33 × 95.35 m in plan and 84.31 m from the lowest foundation point to the top.

The elevation products are ICGC 2021–2023 LiDAR. The oblique survey is from 13 October 2014. Its point elevations were aligned to the LiDAR using a 49.30 m vertical offset observed on the unchanged nave roof. The middle half of the roof samples differed by 49.21–49.39 m before correction. This is a local registration for the measured sections. The guide and conservation study were used to check the architecture. The OSM relation `1012115` version 13 defines the outline used for replacement, including the courtyard opening. The reduced survey fixture keeps the footprint, product URLs, pixel windows and checksums, the registration and the sampled ground and roof envelope. The published footprint and plan data in the fixture are © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. The adapted mesh is CC BY 4.0 with attribution to Open Place Models contributors and ICGC. After installing the pinned catalogue dependencies, run `node scripts/generate-catedral-de-girona.mjs` from the repository root. An optional argument writes the matching GeoJSON feature. The GLB regenerated to SHA-256 `2f4779e86bbd221ea23f7da31bae902739ad9debc82e32b885c7f4cf6c492013`.

## Validation

- The GLB is 56,984 bytes with 2,339 vertices and 4,678 triangles. An independent audit found one watertight, consistently oriented mesh with positive signed volume of 129,459.46 m³, a single closed face ring at each vertex and zero exact coincident vertices or degenerate triangles. The courtyard is the only opening through the full thickness of the mesh.
- Relleu Obert loaded the local catalogue and fitted the model to terrain with OpenStreetMap and ICGC at 1:2,500 and OpenFreeMap at 1:5,000 with a 26° rotation. Each full selection replaced the cathedral volumes from its source and retained nearby buildings. A partial ICGC crop at 1:2,500 and −22° also produced closed building geometry. The courtyard adjustment allows the same selections to keep the basin in their water layer.
- At 1:1,000 the solid spans about 88 × 95 × 84 mm. The rose recess is about 6.9 mm wide and the belfry recesses about 1.8 mm wide. At 1:2,500 the solid spans about 35 × 38 × 34 mm and the same recesses are about 2.8 mm and 0.71 mm wide. The smaller façade openings are about 0.47 mm wide at 1:2,500, near the width of a common 0.4 mm nozzle. Slicing and physical printing remain to be checked.
