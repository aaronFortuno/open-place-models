# Seu Vella de Lleida · r1

The exterior of the old cathedral includes the nave and side aisles, pronounced transept, stepped apses, octagonal crossing tower, attached Canonja, open cloister and Gothic bell tower. Five pointed openings carry through the south cloister gallery. The Castell del Rei (La Suda), the fortification walls and the hill are separate parts of the monumental site. The catalogue footprint covers the cathedral and Relleu Obert supplies the surrounding hill as terrain. [Museum of Catalan History monument dossier](https://drac.cultura.gencat.cat/handle/20.500.12368/36310) · [UNESCO Tentative List site description](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6079/)

## Measurements and reconstruction

- **Plan.** The OSM outline was checked against ICGC's 2025 25 cm orthophoto. Its outer ring follows the irregular church, Canonja and cloister, and the inner ring leaves the open court. The cloister court measures about 26 × 29 m on the plan. The transept projects beyond the nave and the bell tower stands at the south-west corner of the cloister. [Orthophoto](https://www.icgc.cat/es/Geoinformacion-y-mapas/Datos-y-productos/Imagen/Ortofoto-Territorial) · [OSM relation 4008211](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/4008211)
- **Roof levels.** ICGC's 1 m LiDAR surface model places the cloister galleries at about 233.4–233.6 m, the main nave ridge at about 241 m and the crossing cap at 259.39 m. The separate southern chapel cap reaches 251.38 m. The GLB approximates these observations with roof planes and simplified stepped volumes. The thin upper profiles, gables and gallery openings are simplified for printing. [ICGC surface model](https://catalegs.ide.cat/geonetwork/hub-litoral/api/records/model-superficies-lidar-catalunya-1m-2021-2023)
- **Bell tower.** The two octagonal stages, parapet and grouped pinnacles use the published 60 m height. The LiDAR surface places the upper terrace at 274.12 m and samples the crown up to 277.41 m. The simplified pinnacles reach approximately 279.24 m, using the published height above the local 219.24 m ground sample. The narrow upper tips and tracery are approximate. [Museum dossier](https://drac.cultura.gencat.cat/handle/20.500.12368/36310) · [Lleida Turisme](https://www.lleidaturisme.cat/ca/recursos/llibret-top10_v6_cat-i-cast.pdf)
- **Terrain.** The underside is fitted to a 3 m grid of samples from the ICGC 50 cm bare-earth model and embedded by a nominal 3 m. The 342 underside vertices were checked against the source 50 cm window and sit 2.31–3.67 m below sampled ground. The depth varies around the terrace shoulder. The absolute base anchor places the roof heights while keeping the local slope. [ICGC terrain model and vertical reference](https://catalegs.ide.cat/geonetwork/geonetwork/api/records/model-elevacions-terreny-lidar-catalunya-50cm-2021-2023)

## Registration and reuse

The plan anchor is `[0.62665139, 41.61776222]` in WGS84 longitude–latitude order. GLB Y=0 is **210.760 m** orthometric elevation, referenced to EGM08D595. The GLB uses metres, with X east, Y up and Z south. Its extent is 111.82 × 120.98 m in plan and 68.48 m high including the embedded foundation.

The ICGC crops were read on 8 November 2026, with the LiDAR products based on the 2021–2023 coverage. Their source URLs, UTM crop, ground samples and individual roof probes are recorded in [survey-layout.json](survey-layout.json). The mapped footprint uses OSM relation `4008211` version 9 (23 April 2025), outer way `48495190` version 16 and court way `196006368` version 1. La Suda is a separate mapped way, `336837053`. ## Validation

- The local catalogue and print loader accept the 31,272-byte GLB. The independent mesh audit found one closed component, 1,265 vertices and 2,542 triangles, a positive signed volume of 114,656.13 m³ and zero open edges, non-manifold edges, orientation conflicts, duplicate vertices or degenerate triangles. Each vertex has a single incident face ring.
- The geometry's dimensions at 1:2,500 are approximately 44.7 × 48.4 × 27.4 mm in plan and height. At 1:5,000 they are 22.4 × 24.2 × 13.7 mm. The main gallery openings are 5.5 m wide, giving about 2.2 and 1.1 mm respectively. Thin finial tips can disappear at either scale on a 0.4 mm nozzle.
- Relleu Obert loaded the local catalogue and placed the cathedral on captured terrain. At 1:2,500 with OpenStreetMap, the original cathedral outline was replaced and La Suda stayed separate. At 1:5,000 with OpenFreeMap and 27° rotation, the relevant building fragment was replaced. The ICGC tests at 1:2,500 covered the whole model and a partial crop rotated −22°. The resulting building meshes passed the solid audit.
- The 320 × 200 WebP thumbnail was checked visually. Slicing and physical printing remain pending.
