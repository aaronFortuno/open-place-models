# Colosseo · r1

Rome, Italy. The model represents the surviving Flavian Amphitheatre. It includes the three arcades and attic on the northern front, the lower exposed wall on the south, the cavea, and the open hypogeum beside the partial arena platform. The two outer corridors that collapsed on the south side remain absent. The 2026 project marks their former footprint in the pavement. The model covers the standing ruin. [Parco archeologico del Colosseo, southern corridors](https://colosseo.it/ambulacri-meridionali/)

## Geometry and measurements

- **Surviving perimeter.** The plan uses the OpenStreetMap ruin outline and six mapped annular components. The model is 178.3 × 145.0 m along east and north, and approximately 189.7 × 141.5 m along its own axes. The long axis runs about 20° south of east, fitted to the mapped cavea and exposed ring. The original 188 × 156 m amphitheatre gives a reference for spacing the bays. The standing footprint is narrower on the short axis because the southern outer rings have collapsed. [OSM outline and members](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/1834818) · [Texas Tech architectural records](https://exhibits.lib.ttu.edu/s/openarch/item/18831)
- **Arcades and buttresses.** The three northern orders follow the GNDT structural study's 4.3 m bay openings and 7.1, 6.5 and 6.4 m opening heights. The study gives 80 bays per original order. Bay stations are evenly spaced along a reference oval, then placed on the mapped wall. Exact bay positions, attic windows and damaged edges are simplified. Some third-order arches and attic windows pass through the shell. The two lower orders and exposed southern arches are recesses with backing for print. The eastern Stern end keeps the mapped wall termination and the western Valadier end is approximated by a slope. The maximum height is 48.5 m above the flat print base, matching the nominal façade height. [GNDT structural monograph](https://emidius.mi.ingv.it/GNDT2/Pubblicazioni/Liberatore_Beni_Archeologici/Cap1_Liberatore.htm) · [Structural study of the façade](https://www.cni.it/images/il_giornale_dell_ingegnere/2021/Gli_Speciali_de_Il_Giornale_dellIngegnere_1_2021_Il_Colosseo_sulle_ragioni_del_suo_stato_di_danneggiamento.pdf) · [Parco, the north front and buttresses](https://colosseo.it/restauri/colosseo/)
- **Arena and cavea.** The model traces the mapped open arena, eastern platform and hypogeum masonry. The simplified arena outline measures approximately 86.4 × 49.8 m after trimming the axial entrances. The historical architectural record gives an arena of 86 × 54 m. The model floor-to-platform difference is 5.7 m, compared with the approximately 6 m drop documented by the Parco. The six broad seating steps, partly exposed radial walls and the depths of the masonry relief are simplified. The flat plate under the excavated floor joins the walls for printing. [OSM arena](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/19666836) · [OSM eastern platform](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/19666835) · [OSM hypogeum](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/3371742) · [Parco, hypogeum corridors](https://colosseo.it/opere/montacarichi-e-piattaforme/) · [Texas Tech architectural records](https://exhibits.lib.ttu.edu/s/openarch/item/18831)

## Registration, source and reuse

The WGS84 anchor is `[12.49237365, 41.89026025]`. The GLB uses metres with X east, Y up and Z south. The catalogue footprint is OpenStreetMap relation `1834818` version 68. Replacement targets the relation and its own building parts. The Arch of Constantine, the surrounding ruins and other nearby buildings have separate identities. [OpenStreetMap Colosseum](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/1834818)

The footprint and the OSM-derived layout in `osm-layout.json` are © OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL 1.0. The fixture records the relation versions. Its coordinates are local metres east and north of the anchor. The geometry uses simplified mapped sections, an arena trimmed at the axial entrances, cavea offsets and small joins between the separate wall footprints. The resulting printable mesh is CC BY 4.0, credited to Open Place Models contributors with attribution to OpenStreetMap. The public documentation of the official HBIM provided survey context. The geometry is generated from the included layout and architectural dimensions. The separately published CMBC asset was excluded because its capture provenance could not be verified. [OpenStreetMap attribution and ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [Parco integrated survey](https://colosseo.it/en/press_kit/parco-colosseo-3d-hbim-salone-restauro-ferrara/) · [CMBC listing](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/colosseum-rome-italy-535dc96e586f40bd956ea3cbff810055)

After installing the pinned dependencies, run `node scripts/generate-colosseum.mjs` from the repository root. The generator reads the included layout and writes `print.glb`. A second run produced the same SHA-256 `f94625f9aa05fa6524e72b338f034ce656a6e2b2e252c624f8aeb257d8a7613f`.

## Validation

- The GLB is 143,356 bytes with 5,884 vertices, 11,928 triangles and one closed, consistently oriented component. The signed volume is 380,687.88 m³ and the shell has genus 41. Both the generator and an independent edge audit found zero boundary edges, non-manifold edges, orientation conflicts and degenerate triangles. There are no coincident vertices. The overlap of the bottom face and the mapped footprint is 99.76% by intersection over union. Simplifying the mesh more aggressively produced degenerate faces. A milder simplification saved about 1.6 kB, so this revision preserves the arcs, terrace edges and hypogeum traces.
- Local Relleu Obert checks used vector buildings at 1:1,500 and 0°, OSM3D at 1:4,000 and 27°, and a partial crop at 1:1,500 and 9°. The complete tests replaced 8 vector and 11 OSM3D parts, preserving 4 and 53 neighbouring features respectively. Ground contact, height limits and the hidden-buildings mode passed. The audited 3MF at 1:1,500 is 333,750 bytes with 28,588 triangles.
- The local catalogue viewer verified search by alias, zoom, rotation, the reported 143.4 kB size and access to the GLB.
- At 1:1,500 the ruin is about 119 × 97 × 32 mm. At 1:4,000 it is about 45 × 36 × 12 mm. The 4.3 m northern arch openings span about 1.08 mm at 1:4,000. Radial samples of the thin northern wall away from the end buttresses range from 2.12 to 2.68 m, about 0.53–0.67 mm at that scale. Some hypogeum traces are narrower than a 0.4 mm nozzle at 1:4,000 and can merge in a slicer. Slicing and physical printing remain to be checked.
