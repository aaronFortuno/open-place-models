# Basilica di San Marco · r1

Venice, Italy. The model covers the basilica and the mapped sacristy wing. It preserves the Greek-cross plan, five raised lead-covered domes and the two orders of arches facing Piazza San Marco. The adjoining Palazzo Ducale and the freestanding Campanile are separate buildings. Raoul Bedin reports dimensions of 76.50 × 62.60 m for the basilica. The model follows the mapped perimeter, including its rear extension and smaller projections, with an overall east–west and north–south extent of 92.85 × 72.43 m. [Procuratoria façade description](https://www.basilicasanmarco.it/en/sculpture/the-decoration-of-the-facades/) · [Bedin's 2018 survey and boundary map, pp. 2–3](https://globaluserfiles.com/media/226556_b97d1748ff3f8538eb11b775b021f44c1f8bcf65.pdf/o/articolo%20lps%20basilica%20san%20marco.pdf)

## Survey and reconstruction

- **Roof and domes.** The 2014 Città Metropolitana di Venezia surface model supplies the low roof elevations and the five dome centres and radial profiles. The local reference is 1.15 m, the median elevation from the terrain model near the basilica. The highest detected roof returns above that reference are 42.24 m at the central Ascension dome, 39.25 m at the eastern Prophets dome, 37.87 m at the western Pentecost dome, 36.00 m at the southern St Leonard dome and 35.73 m at the northern St John dome. The model uses selected stations from radial medians and approximates the drum and profile transitions with facets. The Prophets dome was scaffolded in 2014, so its shell is interpolated across unreliable parts of the profile. [Venice DSM 2014 and licence](https://geodati.gov.it/geoportale/visualizzazione-metadati/scheda-metadati?metadataid=p_ve:000045:20241031:114741) · [Bedin's report on the Prophets dome](https://globaluserfiles.com/media/226556_b97d1748ff3f8538eb11b775b021f44c1f8bcf65.pdf/o/articolo%20lps%20basilica%20san%20marco.pdf)
- **Terminals.** Bedin places the central weather vane at 50 m and gives a range of approximately 44–47 m for the other four. The model uses 50 m for the central tip and estimates individual tips within the reported range. The historic Visentini section guides the simplified cross profiles above the lanterns. Thin vanes, crossbars and shafts are represented by solid print-sized elements. [Bedin's 2018 dimensions, p. 3](https://globaluserfiles.com/media/226556_b97d1748ff3f8538eb11b775b021f44c1f8bcf65.pdf/o/articolo%20lps%20basilica%20san%20marco.pdf) · [Visentini section reproduced by Rossi, figure 1](https://www.rteknos.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/467.pdf)
- **Piazza façade.** The west façade has five bays at each of its two levels, including four lower portals and the raised window of the Zen Chapel at the southern end. The offsets follow the mapped front. Relative widths, spring lines and crest profiles are estimated from Ongania's measured plates. Bedin's reported 31.30 m statue top and 27.90 m aedicules set the height of the principal crest and pinnacles. Recess depth and thickness are simplified for printing. Individual sculptures, the quadriga, mosaics, fine tracery and interior geometry are omitted. [Procuratoria west façade description](https://www.basilicasanmarco.it/en/sculpture/the-decoration-of-the-facades/) · [Ongania, Portafoglio I, Tav. II 1–3 and Tav. III](https://ongania-sanmarco.it/biblioteca_digitale/1-Portafoglio-1)

## Registration, provenance and reuse

The anchor `[12.339889752201758, 45.4345814019474]` is the fitted centre of the Ascension dome. The GLB uses X east, Y up and Z south, in metres. The building grid follows the fitted east–west dome axis, 14.28° north of east. The footprint uses OpenStreetMap way `138800932` version 60. The local polygon is simplified at 0.24 m before it is used to crop the roof surface. [OpenStreetMap basilica outline](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/138800932)

The `survey-layout.json` fixture records the WCS request, GeoTIFF affine transform and checksum, roof triangles, fitted dome centres and radial samples. The roof is triangulated on a clipped 4.5 m grid from nearby DSM medians. Dome pixels and outliers are masked during this step. The model interpolates roof heights under domes and along the scaffolded frontage, and excludes returns above the low sacristy roof where it meets adjacent geometry. The studies by the Politecnico di Milano and Procuratoria describe a 1:50 survey and HBIM. The project page provides publications and a contact. The mesh here is regenerated from the documented survey measurements and parameters in the fixture. After installing the pinned dependencies, run `node scripts/generate-st-marks-basilica.mjs` from the repository root. ## Validation

- The GLB is 58,208 bytes with 2,373 vertices and 4,742 triangles in a single closed component. The signed volume is 88,786.25 m³. The audit found zero boundary edges, orientation conflicts, degenerate faces, disconnected incident face rings or coincident positions. Horizontal sections show five distinct domes at 28 m and the single central terminal at 48 m. The default retains the radial sections and two levels of façade relief above the catalogue's 20–30 kB target.
- Local Relleu Obert checks used vector buildings at 1:1,000 and 0°, OSM3D at 1:2,500 and 27°, and a partial crop at 1:1,000 and 9°. They confirmed terrain contact, replacement of the basilica parts, and preservation of neighbouring buildings, including the Palazzo Ducale and Campanile. The final 3MF passed its audit with 342,367 bytes and 18,870 triangles. The local catalogue build and viewer checks covered search by alias, size, rotation, zoom and access to the GLB.
- At 1:1,000 and 1:2,500 the model stands 50 mm and 20 mm respectively, before the terrain. Crossbar spans are 3.2 mm and 1.28 mm. The thinnest cross shafts and Gothic tips can be narrower than a common extrusion line at 1:2,500. Physical slicing and printing remain to be checked.
