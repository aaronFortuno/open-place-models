# Cremallera de Núria · Roc del Dui–Núria · r1

The detailed section follows 2,983.9 m of the active rack railway from the north portal of the Roc del Dui tunnel to the end of the main track at Núria. It includes the Fontalba and Fontnegra tunnels, the Pal Tort gallery, the tunnel into Núria, and the bridges over Roc Roig and Tres Molins. The main track ends at the station buffers. Relleu Obert also retains the two connected terminal branches. The former alignment is separate from the active track. [FGC route description and station layout, pp. 4–5](https://contractaciopublica.cat/portal-api/descarrega-document/1329761/3012731EB8F1723B79458A0AB5903822)

## Regional model

`route.json` supplies a continuous base alignment from Ribes to Núria. Relleu Obert v0.23.46 or later uses it in Model complet. It preserves the same centreline and elevation on both sides of each catalogue joint, including the full Roc del Dui tunnel south of the detailed strip. The 60 paths are derived from 63 current ICGC features. At the Núria station switches, the main-track path combines ICGC IDs 1250, 648, 647 and 649. The other terminal paths retain IDs 646 and 1253. The ICGC elevated segments 1246 and 1256 supply the spans at Roc Roig and Tres Molins, about 17 and 16 m. Twenty-two OpenFreeMap railway IDs were checked against the same alignment or its mapped branches and are listed for replacement. Other transport remains in the general layer. [ICGC RTT and licence](https://www.icgc.cat/ca/Geoinformacio-i-mapes/Dades-i-productes/Geoinformacio-cartografica/Referencial-Topografic-Territorial) · [Underground](https://maps.icgc.cat/vector01/rest/services/topografia_territorial/MapServer/24) · [Surface and elevated](https://maps.icgc.cat/vector01/rest/services/topografia_territorial/MapServer/25) · [OpenStreetMap attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)

Plan coordinates come from ETRS89 / UTM zone 31N and are stored as WGS84. Elevations are estimated in EGM08D595 from the ICGC 2021–2023 lidar terrain, resampled at 5 m in the north and 8 m farther south. The surface fit uses the local terrain, shared endpoints and a limited grade. Bridge and tunnel elevations are interpolated between fitted abutments and portals. The terrain fit at the north portal of Roc del Dui uses 1,631.3 m to meet the visible ledge in the raster. The FGC technical annex reports 1,649 m for the north portal. The two values should be checked against a longitudinal survey if one becomes available. The 4 m running width, arched bores, supports and clearances are printing approximations. Pal Tort uses the mapped covered alignment and a simplified opening. Fine track components, including the rack, sleepers, overhead wiring and tunnel sidings, are outside this revision. [ICGC lidar terrain](https://datacloud.icgc.cat/datacloud/model-elevacions-terreny/tif_unzip/model-elevacions-terreny-lidar-catalunya-50cm-2021-2023.tif) · [FGC structural tunnel inventory, p. 5](https://contractaciopublica.cat/portal-api/descarrega-document-antic/109884094/45f2c47864e2c4ae0cd25fcf876000dd)

Enable estimated tunnels in Model complet to see the openings. The engine widens small bores to the printable minimum and checks roof cover and fit to the rendered terrain. Closer crops show the structures and station switches more clearly. The centreline and the three terminal paths retain their full geographic extent across selections.

## Local Relleu Obert controls

- North portal of Roc del Dui and Fontalba at 1:1,600 (+11°). The two bores joined their mapped landings. All 236 sampled track-surface points were present.
- South portal of Roc del Dui at 1:1,800 (−19°). Both ends of the tunnel matched the adjacent ICGC route. All 250 visible probes across the tunnel and the base railway passed.
- Roc Roig at 1:2,000 (−11°) and Tres Molins at 1:1,500 (+9°). Both abutments of each bridge joined the mapped railway, with zero unconnected ends. Fontnegra also opened with both mapped connections at 1:1,600 (−17°).
- Arrival at Núria at 1:2,400 (+13°). The final arrival crop passed all 639 probes on the tunnel floor, main track and both terminal paths.
- An overview at 1:6,600 generated both railway decks and the five covered runs including Roc del Dui, with closed material meshes. It exposed a gap at the coarse terrain under the Núria tunnel. After the fix, the dedicated 55 × 57 mm crop at the same scale passed 265 probes across the whole tunnel and its two landings. The final full-size overview rerun was deferred because of available memory.

## Standalone gallery model

The GLB represents the main track on a 30 m wide terrain strip, with a short cutaway into the Roc del Dui at the southern end. The survey fixture samples six terrain points across 234 cross-sections. Its 4,046 triangles form one closed solid. The mesh weighs 49,196 bytes and the local origin is at [2.15974887, 42.37355841], with Y=0 at 1,626 m EGM08D595. Ray tests confirm clearance below both decks, air in all five bore runs, and open sky at eight exposed stations. The regional sidecar and the gallery GLB are separate files.
