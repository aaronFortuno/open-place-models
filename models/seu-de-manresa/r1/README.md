# Seu de Manresa · r1

This exterior reconstruction covers the Basilica of Santa Maria de la Seu on the Puigcardener. It includes the flat nave, stepped aisles, polygonal chevet, flying buttresses, square bell tower and the volumes of the west porch, Santíssim chapel and octagonal baptistery. The rector's buildings, cloisters, approach stairs and retaining walls are outside this revision. [Basilica architecture](https://seudemanresa.cat/arquitectura/) · [Manresa municipal heritage inventory, R005](https://web.manresa.cat/media/docs/arxius/doc_contingut_10578.pdf)

## Measurements and reconstruction

- **Nave, aisles and chevet.** The municipal inventory describes the temple as 68 × 33 m, with a nave about 30 m high. The model plan follows the orthophoto and OSM outline, which also includes projections such as the west chapel and baptistery. The axis from front to chevet is 20.08° north of UTM grid east. The central roof is set at 268.90 m from the ICGC surface returns, and the lower roof at about 255.9 m. The ambulatory steps around the heptagonal chevet. A small octagonal stair tower at the east end uses its local surface maximum of about 270.7 m. [Manresa inventory](https://web.manresa.cat/media/docs/arxius/doc_contingut_10578.pdf) · [ICGC elevation models](https://www.icgc.cat/ca/Geoinformacio-i-mapes/Dades-i-productes/Elevacions/Elevacions-territorial/Models-delevacions)
- **Buttresses and openings.** The inventory records 18 flying buttresses. The model places eight on the south, six on the north and four around the chevet, following visible roof traces and mapped pier projections. The arch profiles are simplified from the exterior views, with open undersides and widths of 1.26–1.39 m. Recesses represent the nave lancets and west rose. Their position and width are estimates from the exterior rather than dimensions from a measured elevation. [Manresa inventory](https://web.manresa.cat/media/docs/arxius/doc_contingut_10578.pdf) · [Basilica exterior and structural photographs](https://seudemanresa.cat/arquitectura/)
- **Campanar and west front.** The bell tower has an approximately 9.5 m square shaft, two pointed openings per face and a parapet. The crown is 289.65 m from the measured surface, about 50 m above the surrounding terrain, consistent with the published 50.5 m height. The 20th-century porch and baptistery associated with Alexandre Soler i March are represented by basic volumes, the three porch openings and the taper of the baptistery. The proportions come from the cartography and visible exterior. The generator uses original geometry. Carvings, railings, glazing tracery, interior fittings and artworks are omitted. [Basilica architecture](https://seudemanresa.cat/arquitectura/) · [Manresa inventory](https://web.manresa.cat/media/docs/arxius/doc_contingut_10578.pdf) · [Bell tower survey](https://campaners.com/php/torrepdf.php?numer=2042)

## Registration, source and reuse

The WGS84 anchor is `[1.82723631326, 41.72192424820]`. The GLB uses metres with X east, Y up and Z south. Its underside follows the 0.5 m ICGC terrain with a 4.6 m offset below the sampled surface. The embedment includes an extra 2.3 m for differences found with the 5 m terrain in Relleu Obert. The lowest foundation point, Y=0, is 228.352 m orthometric on the Alicante datum. The model uses `anchorElevation` in Relleu Obert so the roofs keep their registered altitude while the surrounding terrain retains its slope. The solid spans 85.59 × 59.59 m east–west and north–south and 61.298 m from the lowest foundation point to the crown.

OSM way `383196253` version 6 defines the replacement footprint. ICGC feature `icgc-1530154` is entirely within the temple outline and is also eligible for direct replacement. Feature `icgc-895396` covers part of the temple and substantial neighbouring buildings, so it has no direct replacement ID. The reduced survey fixture records the 2021–2023 LiDAR crops at 1 m and 0.5 m resolution, the orthophoto request, the registration and the simplified base geometry. ## Validation

- The GLB is 43,548 bytes with 1,762 vertices and 3,592 triangles. An independent audit found one watertight, consistently oriented solid with positive signed volume of 75,844.59 m³ and zero duplicate positions, degenerate triangles or disconnected face rings. Its topology preserves 18 openings. A vertical section at each flyer's midpoint found a gap of at least 6.64 m above the aisle roof. The flying arches and recessed windows account for the file size above the usual catalogue target.
- Relleu Obert fitted the complete OSM selection at 1:1,500 on the 5 m terrain. At the anchor the foundation lies at 234.23 m and the terrain at 235.86 m, and the crown is at 289.65 m. The cathedral way was replaced and the neighbouring relation `5700895` and way `383197778` were retained. Limited-height and hidden-building modes passed. The complete OpenFreeMap selection at 1:3,000 and 26° replaced both fragments of `3831962532`. A partial OSM crop at 1:2,000 and −19°, shifted 98 m east, produced closed geometry. The ICGC selection at 1:1,500 replaced `icgc-1530154` and kept the fused `icgc-895396` to retain its neighbouring buildings. Some overlapping procedural geometry remains with that source.
- At 1:1,000 the solid spans about 86 × 60 × 61 mm. At 1:2,500 it spans about 34 × 24 × 25 mm and the flyers are about 0.50–0.56 mm thick. At 1:4,000 they are about 0.32–0.35 mm thick and may disappear with a 0.4 mm nozzle. Slicing and physical printing remain to be checked.
