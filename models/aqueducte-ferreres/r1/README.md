# Aqüeducte de les Ferreres · r1

This reconstruction shows the Pont del Diable at Tarragona with 25 upper arches, 11 lower arches, the stepped lower piers and the open conduit. The 5.90 m openings, 7.95 m centres and 1.86 m width of the upper masonry follow the archaeological description. The lower tier sits beneath 11 consecutive upper bays. The model keeps two steps in the lower piers and omits individual stones and the springing mouldings. [Mar et al., 2015, fig. 31 and pp. 56–60](https://www.icac.cat/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/TARRACO_II_Arquitectura_y_urbanismo_de_una1.pdf#page=58) · [Ajuntament de Tarragona](https://www.tarragona.cat/patrimoni/patrimoni-mundial/conjunt-arqueologic-de-tarraco-1/museu-historia/monuments/laqueducte-del-francoli)

## Registration

The 236.026 m model extent includes the masonry and the two landing margins visible on the current orthophoto. The municipality gives 217 m for the aqueduct. The arcade spacing comes from the archaeological study and the north–south placement follows the orthophoto and the mapped axis. The simplified conduit runs level at 57.66 m and the parapet crest is at 58.54 m EGM08D595, estimated from the 2021–23 surface model. The intermediate cornice at 47.18 m follows exposed surface returns beside the upper shafts. The piers follow the 0.5 m terrain model, with 1.15 m of extra depth for contact with coarser terrain in a print. The GLB origin is `[1.243880570, 41.145951281]` at 57.66 m. X points east, Y up and Z south. ## Validation

- The GLB is 32,716 bytes, with 1,320 vertices and 2,684 triangles. Its indexed shells are the continuous masonry and the channel floor. Both are closed and the masonry alone preserves all 36 openings. Relleu Obert loads the masonry prefix and replaces the stand-alone floor with its georeferenced bridge surface.
- Relleu Obert produced one connected bridge in the 1:1,500 (+7°) and 1:2,500 (−16°) full crops and in the 1:1,500 (+13°) crop through the arcade. The full checks found the 23 upper openings above the modeled terrain, all 11 lower openings and ground contact at the 10 interior lower piers. The cut retained 16 upper openings and capped the two cut ends.
- With the captured OpenFreeMap paths and ICGC MET5, Relleu Obert formed a smooth path connection at one end in both full crops. Its optional procedural landing supports were skipped. The lower piers are part of the mesh and passed the ground-contact check. The channel and parapets are thin at smaller scales and the arch crowns may require support on a physical print.
