# Catedral de Barcelona · r2

Barcelona, Spain. This revision keeps the municipal plan and open cloister courtyard. It raises the nave side terraces to the measured level, restores the shallow roof slopes and simplified buttresses, and replaces the pointed caps of the two medieval bell towers with recessed octagonal terraces. The clock tower retains its slender upper frame as a solid profile. The two façade pinnacles now have the same measured height. The main spire and front gable retain their silhouette with simplified masonry and portal relief. [Cathedral architecture](https://catedralbcn.org/la-catedral/edifici/) · [Restoration overview](https://catedralbcn.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Obert-per-obres-Catedral-de-Barcelona.pdf) · [Eduardo Henrique's exterior view on Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cathedral-of-bcnsanta-creu-and-santa-eulalia-4395e17feb95400c891597121a59513f)

## Survey and reconstruction

The plan and base anchor reuse the municipal CAD subset in [revision 1](../r1/README.md). The height study clips the 1 km ICGC LiDAR tile `431581` to the cathedral, in ETRS89 / UTM 31N. Section heights use the same 12 m common ground plane as the CAD model. The measurements show nave centre medians near 29–30 m, side terraces near 24 m, façade pinnacle tips near 47.4 m and bell tower terrace floors near 54 m. The parapets reach about 56 m and the clock tower frame about 68 m. The highest returns on the central cross are 77.4–78.7 m above the model datum. The mesh uses 78 m for that tip. The cathedral's architectural summary lists 70 m for the cimbori without identifying its datum. [ICGC LiDAR Territorial](https://www.icgc.cat/ca/Geoinformacio-i-mapes/Dades-i-productes/Elevacions/Elevacions-territorial/LiDAR-Territorial) · [Source tile](https://datacloud.icgc.cat/datacloud/lidar-territorial/laz_unzip/full10km4358/lidar-territorial-v3r1-full1km431581-2021-2023.laz) · [Cathedral dimensions](https://catedralbcn.org/la-catedral/edifici/)

`survey.json` records the tile checksum, measurement datum, class selection and model dimensions. The roof slopes, buttresses and tower stages approximate sampled sections. The bell chamber openings, metalwork, tracery and sculpture are consolidated or omitted for small prints. The Sketchfab model is a visual reference for distinguishing the main volumes. The mesh derives from the municipal footprints, LiDAR measurements and original modelling. To reproduce it, install the pinned dependencies and run `node scripts/generate-barcelona-cathedral.mjs r2` from the repository root.

## Validation

- The GLB is 28,508 bytes, with 1,148 vertices and 2,296 triangles in one closed component. Its signed volume is about 130,095 m³. The courtyard stays open and the exterior extends to 78 m above the shared base.
- The comparison used the median of at least three building-class LiDAR returns per 1 m cell where both meshes cover the same roof. Mean absolute height differences against those samples fell from 1.04 m to 0.40 m on the central nave (476 cells), from 11.29 m to 0.64 m on the side terraces (509 cells), and from 1.69 m to 0.27 m on the tested cloister gallery (201 cells). These samples compare the model against its survey input.
- Local Relleu Obert controls used ICGC at 1:1,000, Catastre and original OSM at 1:2,500, and a partial cut at 1:1,000. The tests covered ground contact, replacement, neighbours, the building-height limit and hiding buildings. The audited 3MF is 75,660 bytes and has 6,032 triangles. The model is 78 mm and 31.2 mm high at the tested scales before adding the terrain. Slicing and physical printing remain to be checked.
