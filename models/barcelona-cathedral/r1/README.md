# Catedral de Barcelona · r1

Barcelona, Spain. This model covers the church and adjoining Gothic cloister, with the courtyard open. It retains the stepped nave terraces, rounded apse, two octagonal bell towers, main cimbori and two lateral pinnacles. The nave, bell towers and cross above the cimbori reach 28 m, 54 m and 70 m respectively. The published 93 × 40 m dimensions describe the church. The complete model, including the cloister, spans approximately 116.35 m east to west and 103.40 m north to south. [Cathedral architecture and dimensions](https://catedralbcn.org/la-catedral/edifici/) · [Rooftop and cloister](https://catedralbcn.org/visita-turistica/espais-visitables/)

## Survey and reconstruction

- The horizontal outlines come from 30 extruded objects in the Ajuntament de Barcelona’s 20 May 2026 Barri Gòtic CAD export. Its municipal roof heights are flat. We retained the lower terraces and cloister walls, adjusted the central nave terrace to the published height, and reconstructed the Gothic pinnacles, bell tower caps, front gable, entrance and rose-window relief, and the cross above the cimbori. Low pitched sections above two cloister galleries follow the roof types visible in the ICGC territorial orthophoto. Decorative tracery and interior geometry are omitted. [Municipal CAD product and licence](https://portaldades.ajuntament.barcelona.cat/mapes/146) · [Territorial orthophoto](https://www.icgc.cat/ca/Geoinformacio-i-mapes/Dades-i-productes/Imatge/Ortofoto-Territorial)
- The ground anchor `[2.17647239276994, 41.38400912241228]` corresponds to `[431143.3, 4581714.3]` in ETRS89 / UTM 31N. The export uses a common local ground plane, with municipal roof elevations referenced to 12 m in the CAD. The GLB is in metres with X east, Y up and Z south. The mapped footprint and courtyard ring follow OSM relation `3298476` version 30. Replacement also identifies `icgc-1200259` and cadastral family `ES.SDGC.BU.1219101DF3811G`, which represent the same complex. [OSM outline](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/3298476)

The catalogue outline from OpenStreetMap retains ODbL 1.0. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [OpenStreetMap attribution and ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)

The cropped `municipal-layout.json` records the DWG object handles, original elevations, footprints and local coordinate conversion. It was extracted from `Barri_02_MTM_DWG.dwg` using LibreDWG 0.14. The retained upper-face footprints were at least 94% inside the mapped complex. The archive’s SHA-256 is recorded in the file. The generator uses that subset and the reconstructed profiles to reproduce the mesh. Run `node scripts/generate-barcelona-cathedral.mjs` from the repository root after installing the pinned dependencies. ## Validation

- The GLB is 22,248 bytes and contains 890 vertices and 1,780 triangles in a single closed component. The signed volume is 109,307 m³. The audit found no boundary edges, edges shared by more than two faces, orientation conflicts, zero-area faces or disconnected incident face rings. One pair of indices retains a coincident position at a base seam 0.4 m above the local ground plane. A horizontal section confirms the open cloister courtyard.
- Local Relleu Obert checks used real ICGC data at 1:1,000, Catastre at 1:2,500 and 28°, original OSM at 1:2,500, and a partial crop at 1:1,000 and 9°. They checked terrain contact, replacement of the cathedral while retaining neighbouring buildings, and the height limit and hidden-building controls. The ICGC 3MF control was 69,623 bytes with 5,516 triangles and passed the standard audit.
- The cimbori is 70 mm tall at 1:1,000 and 28 mm at 1:2,500 before adding the terrain. The 1.1 m crossbar thickness becomes 1.1 mm and 0.44 mm respectively. The silhouette and courtyard were checked at both scales. Physical printing and slicer behaviour remain to be checked.
