# Castell de Cardona · r1

The model covers the fortified crown of the castle hill. It includes the truncated Torre de la Minyona, the Pati d'Armes, the Pati Ducal and its surrounding wings, the cloister by Sant Vicenç, the church and the main walls of the seven-bastion system. Its ground surface joins the terraces and bastions to the surrounding terrain. The outer covered approach to the town, the salt mine, nearby buildings and the castle interiors are outside this revision. [Municipal monument guide](https://www.cardona.cat/arees/359-turisme/el-castell-i-la-collegiata-de-sant-vicenc) · [Generalitat plan and guide](https://patrimoni.gencat.cat/ca/monuments/content/download/726/file/Guia%20visita%20catal%C3%A0)

## Measurements and reconstruction

- **Torre de la Minyona and Pati d'Armes.** The 2006 archaeological survey describes the current truncated tower as about 14 m across at the base and 10 m across at the crown, with a height of 12.5 m from the court or 16.4 m from the foot of the bastion. The model takes the diameter and taper from that survey and the registered elevations from the LiDAR and orthophoto. The deck and parapet are near 583.7 and 584.7 m respectively, and the nearby court is about 573.6 m. The survey's nominal absolute elevation for the court differs from the current LiDAR, so its relative measurements guide the form. The external stair is simplified to stepped volumes. [2006 archaeological survey, pp. 5–7](https://calaix.gencat.cat/bitstream/handle/10687/24062/qmem6007.pdf?sequence=648) · [OSM tower](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/155044258)
- **Sant Vicenç.** The Generalitat guide gives an approximate plan of 51 × 23.5 m, with three aisles, a transept, a crossing tower and three semicircular apses. The OSM outline and orthophoto set the plan. The simplified nave ridge is at 586.87 m, the central apse roof at 583.05 m and the crossing at the observed maximum of 591.731 m. The roofs use simple planes fitted to the LiDAR returns. The apse roofs and octagonal crossing are reconstructed. Small roof furniture, Lombard arcading and interior elements are omitted. [Generalitat guide, entries 7, 11 and 12](https://patrimoni.gencat.cat/ca/monuments/content/download/726/file/Guia%20visita%20catal%C3%A0) · [OSM church](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/155044257)
- **Palace and courts.** The surrounding wings follow the terracotta roof blocks in the orthophoto and the main elevations observed in the surface raster. The Pati Ducal is open to the sky and uses the hole in the OSM Parador relation, with a floor at about 568.3 m. The smaller open cloister next to the church is simplified to four enclosing volumes. [OSM Parador](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/2652263) · [Generalitat guide, entries 4 and 6](https://patrimoni.gencat.cat/ca/monuments/content/download/726/file/Guia%20visita%20catal%C3%A0)
- **Bastions and terrain.** Seven plan polygons and selected connecting walls are based on mapped fortifications and checked against the ICGC orthophoto. Their simplified crest heights use local surface samples, while the lower faces extend into the sloping ground. The ground follows the 0.5 m ICGC terrain and includes a small inward offset in height to integrate into the model terrain. The underside is embedded by 7.8 m relative to the local ICGC terrain. This produces one connected solid and accommodates differences between the 0.5 m survey and coarser map terrain. Small sentry boxes, embrasures and road furniture are omitted. [Generalitat guide, entry 19](https://patrimoni.gencat.cat/ca/monuments/content/download/726/file/Guia%20visita%20catal%C3%A0) · [ICGC elevation products](https://www.icgc.cat/ca/Geoinformacio-i-mapes/Dades-i-productes/Elevacions/Elevacions-territorial/Models-delevacions)

## Registration, source and reuse

The WGS84 anchor is `[1.68564186395, 41.91429671057]`. The GLB uses metres with X east, Y up and Z south. Its lowest point, Y=0, is at 499.30 m orthometric on the Alicante datum. Relleu Obert can apply this `anchorElevation` while keeping the surrounding hill from the selected terrain source. The solid spans 285.62 × 192.69 m east–west and north–south and rises 92.431 m from the foundation datum to the top of the crossing.

The replacement outline follows the fortified crown. It retains cutouts around the independent buildings in OSM ways `155044267`, `1531247718`, `1531247719` and `180756447`. The church, tower, palace and mapped bastions have explicit replacement IDs. The natural slopes outside the fortifications remain supplied by the map terrain. [OSM church](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/155044257) · [OSM tower](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/155044258) · [OSM palace](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/2652263)

The source fixture records the captured 1 m ICGC surface, 0.5 m terrain and orthophoto, the sampled terrain and wall profiles and the original OSM plans and versions. ## Validation

- The GLB is 58,716 bytes with 2,410 vertices and 4,824 triangles. The independent audit found one watertight, consistently oriented shell with positive signed volume of 323,739.19 m³ and zero duplicate positions, degenerate triangles or disconnected face rings. Combining wall segments and simplifying the terrain surface reduced the initial mesh size by 16%, while retaining the same extent and summit. The footprint and elevation changes across the bastions and terraces account for the file size above the usual catalogue target.
- Vertical checks reached the open floors of the Pati Ducal, the cloister and the Pati d'Armes at 568.34 m, 568.74 m and 573.65 m respectively. They also confirmed the tower deck and parapet at 583.72 m and 584.72 m, the 591.731 m crossing and solid walls reaching the ground below all three apses.
- Relleu Obert fitted the whole castle with OpenStreetMap at 1:2,200 and 8° of rotation, replacing ten verified volumes and keeping the four neighbouring buildings. The 5 m terrain was 565.74 m under the crossing, which kept its base at 555.1 m and summit at 591.731 m. Flat and hidden-building modes passed. In the ICGC test at 1:3,300 and −12°, the number of fragments with the Parador's vector group ID `26522630` dropped from ten to three. The three retained fragments lie outside the replacement footprint. The summit remained at the same elevation. A partial crop at 1:1,800 and 19° cut through the castle and produced closed geometry.
- At 1:2,200 the whole piece spans about 130 × 88 × 42 mm including the terrain insert. The 10 m Minyona crown is about 4.5 mm across and the 1 m parapet is about 0.45 mm thick. At 1:4,500 the crown is about 2.2 mm across and the parapet about 0.22 mm thick, which a 0.4 mm nozzle may omit. Slicing and physical printing remain to be checked.
