# Cova Simanya · r1

This revision models the southeast-facing natural entrance of Simanya Gran and the first stretch of the main gallery toward the northwest. The geological inventory describes a keyhole-shaped section. The 2020–21 excavation report shows a pinched base, a wider midsection and an uneven crown in its exterior and interior photos. The original reconstruction uses an estimated height of 11.1 m and a maximum width of 5.5 m, followed by a rising floor and a tapering vault to 10.75 m from the anchor. The side passage, sediment detail and the metal gate installed in 2021 are outside the scope of the reconstruction. [Excavation report, pp. 16–20 and 110–112](https://calaix.gencat.cat/bitstream/handle/10687/457430/qmem16766.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1) · [Geological inventory, p. 4](https://parcs.diba.cat/documents/215256/365f21f1-bb36-44d8-85ac-4e30ddf5871e) · [1966 ERE survey and description](https://espeleoworld.com/c/cova-simanya) · [Natural park virtual tour](https://parcs.diba.cat/ca/web/santllorenc/visita-virtual-360)

The anchor is [2.00904385, 41.6741292] in WGS84, equivalent to [417513.5, 4614070.5] in ETRS89 / UTM 31N. It lies between the Diputació and OpenStreetMap entrance markers, at the incision in the ICGC terrain. The 0.5 m DTM gives 879.27 m EGM08D595 at the anchor and climbs to about 896 m within the first 4 m toward the northwest. The excavation report's regional site marker is 14.7 m northeast of the model anchor and samples 873.13 m on the DTM. The anchor, 135° outward azimuth and loft sections are an interpretation based on the inventories, orthophoto, terrain and surveyed gallery direction. [Diputació inventory](https://patrimonicultural.diba.cat/element/cova-simanya-cova-simanya-gran) · [OpenStreetMap entrance marker](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1800422295) · [ICGC orthophoto](https://www.icgc.cat/ca/Geoinformacio-i-mapes/Geoinformacio-en-linia-Geoserveis/WMS-Ortoimatges/WMS-Ortofoto-Territorial) · [ICGC DTM and CC BY 4.0 terms](https://catalegs.ide.cat/geonetwork/geonetwork/api/records/model-elevacions-terreny-lidar-catalunya-50cm-2021-2023?language=cat)

The sidecar contains the original closed cutter and a 72 × 72 m elevation grid. The gallery GLB shows a 24 × 24 m section trimmed at 901 m. Both contain ICGC elevations under CC BY 4.0. The gallery mesh has 2,082 triangles and occupies 25.74 kB.

Relleu Obert loaded the model from the local catalogue at 1:600, at 1:1,200 with a 22° rotation and in a partial 1:1,000 selection rotated −15°. All three passed the solid audit. Rays crossed the mouth and hit the roof, floor, back and both side walls. The estimated 11.1 m height corresponds to 18.5 mm at 1:600 and 9.25 mm at 1:1,200 before any terrain clipping. The 1.8 m useful width used for scale selection corresponds to 3.0 and 1.5 mm at those scales. At 1:2,400 it drops to 0.75 mm, below the engine's 0.8 mm threshold, so the cutter is omitted. Printing and support behavior can be assessed from an exported selection.

To regenerate using the committed elevations and cutter profile:

```sh
node scripts/generate-cavity.mjs cova-simanya
python scripts/render-thumbnail.py models/cova-simanya/r1/print.glb --camera 1 .07 .25 --light -.1 .6 .79
```

To rebuild the grid from the source COG, sample the 72 m square at 97 × 97 points with 0.75 m spacing in true east and north at the anchor. Use bilinear interpolation of the 0.5 m pixel centres. Round to centimetres, order rows north to south and columns west to east, and pass a JSON object containing `anchor`, `side`, `step` and `elevations` as the last generator argument.
