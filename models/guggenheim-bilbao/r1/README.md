# Museo Guggenheim Bilbao · r1

A simplified exterior of Frank Gehry’s museum, including the central flower-shaped atrium crown, the curved titanium volumes and the rectilinear limestone wings. The long eastern body represents Gallery 104 and its passage under La Salve Bridge. The bridge deck, the separate Torre de La Salve, the riverside canopy, Puppy and the other outdoor artworks are outside the model. The gallery preview uses a plain material. [Museum exterior](https://www.guggenheim-bilbao.eus/el-edificio/el-exterior) · [Building materials and construction](https://www.guggenheim-bilbao.eus/el-edificio/la-construccion)

## Geometry and placement

- The 1 m geoEuskadi elevation crops cover the full building. The current surface crop returned the same bytes as the explicitly dated 2012 LiDAR product for this area. The observed roof maximum was 57.82 m EGM08_REDNAP at UTM 30N `[505354.5, 4790673.5]`. The riverside approach is about 4.5–5.5 m and the higher south plaza reaches about 14.6 m within the mapped perimeter. The museum’s published 55 m interior atrium height is a separate reference. [geoEuskadi elevation products](https://www.geo.euskadi.eus/modelo-digital-elevaciones-1-metro-del-pais-vasco/webgeo00-dataset/es/) · [Published atrium height](https://www.guggenheim-bilbao.eus/apoyo-empresarial/eventos-corporativos/atrio)
- The roof envelope comes from filtered surface samples triangulated on a 3 m grid, retaining the measured summit. The volume simplifies the overlaps between roof panels and adds a small wall inset beneath the curved cladding. These wall sections are interpretive. The lower envelope follows the terrain at approximately 2.15 m of embedment before simplification. [geoEuskadi raster service](https://www.euskadi.eus/servicio-de-informacion-raster-de-pendientes-orientaciones-y-elevaciones-de-la-capv/web01-s2hhome/es/) · [Museum exterior](https://www.guggenheim-bilbao.eus/el-edificio/el-exterior)
- The geographic footprint follows OSM way `118499485` version 50 with a local clip to separate eastern outdoor terraces from the gallery. The orthoimage and bridge outline were used to distinguish the roadway and stairs from the museum. Where the bridge occludes the roof, a low envelope around 17–19 m absolute is estimated from the adjoining unoccluded gallery sections. The museum’s description confirms the connection under the bridge. [OSM museum outline](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/118499485) · [OSM bridge outline](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1017092845) · [Museum description of Gallery 104](https://www.guggenheim-bilbao.eus/el-edificio/el-exterior)
- The catalogue outline and the mesh plan are trimmed along two reflecting pools. The generalised OpenFreeMap pool boundaries intersected the unadjusted building outline by about 30.1 m². The catalogue outline has at least 0.31 m clearance. Mesh simplification leaves about 0.23 m² of horizontal projection over the south pool at its edge. [South reflecting pool](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/118499484) · [North reflecting pool](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/171881385)

The WGS84 anchor is `[-2.93406129499, 43.26842817013]`. The baked GLB uses metres with X east, Y up and Z south. Its Y=0 corresponds to 1.801 m EGM08_REDNAP, set separately as `anchorElevation` to retain the measured ground slope. The mesh spans approximately 219.76 × 145.31 m in plan and 56.019 m from its lowest foundation point to the roof summit.

## Validation

- The GLB is 37,876 bytes with 1,543 vertices and 3,082 triangles. The independent audit found one watertight, consistently oriented solid with positive signed volume, zero coincident vertices or degenerate faces and a single face ring at every vertex.
- Against 8,513 LiDAR surface pixels at least 2 m inside the footprint and west of the bridge section, the median absolute roof difference is 0.69 m and the 90th percentile is 2.54 m. The comparison excludes ground-height returns. Abrupt transitions between panels are simplified.
- Relleu Obert passed three local controls with closed output meshes. At 1:1,800 the original OSM footprint and 20 of its museum parts were replaced, the independent canopy was preserved, and the limited-height and hidden-building modes passed. The OpenFreeMap control at 1:4,000 and 26° removed the overlapping museum fragments and retained the canopy. The partial OSM crop at 1:2,400 and −19°, shifted 80 m east, retained a closed cut.
- The roof heights remain fixed in absolute metres. At 1:1,500 the full mesh spans about 147 × 97 × 37 mm, including its embedded base. At 1:3,000 it spans about 73 × 49 × 19 mm. Slicing and physical printing remain to be checked.

Run `node scripts/generate-guggenheim-bilbao.mjs` from the repository root after installing the pinned dependencies. An optional path writes the catalogue feature. `survey-layout.json` stores the source requests and checksums, the georeferenced outline and the registered roof and foundation samples.
