# Palace of Westminster · r1

An original exterior reconstruction of the Houses of Parliament. It covers the riverside wings, the ten principal mapped courts, Westminster Hall, St Stephen’s Hall and Porch, the octagonal Central Tower and Victoria Tower. Relleu Obert places the separate [Elizabeth Tower model](../../elizabeth-tower/r1/README.md) in its fitted corner at the north end. [UK Parliament’s architectural overview](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/building/palace/architecture/palacestructure/new-gothic-vision/) · [Palace dimensions and setting](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/building/palace/architecture/the-palace-today/)

## Geometry and placement

- Victoria Tower is 98.5 m above nominal grade to the crown and 120.5 m to the flagstaff tip. The flagstaff is thickened for printing. The tower retains its four corner pinnacles, layered roof and a deep pointed recess at the Sovereign’s Entrance. The Central Tower rises 91.4 m above grade, with an octagonal shaft, corner pinnacles and tapered stone spire. [Victoria Tower dimensions](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/building/palace/architecture/palacestructure/victoria-tower-project/) · [Central Tower dimensions](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/building/palace/architecture/palacestructure/towers-of-parliament/)
- The longitudinal ranges and cross wings follow measured ridge bands from the Environment Agency’s 1 m surface raster. Westminster Hall keeps its pitched roof and gabled north entrance. The repeated river-front pinnacles and window recesses represent the exterior rhythm at model scale. The roof subdivisions and ornamental details are approximate. [Westminster Hall roof](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/building/palace/westminsterhall/architecture/the-hammer-beam-roof-/) · [Environment Agency DSM](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/cf3f1137-c12b-44a1-a835-e80fe4a60b92/lidar-composite-dsm-2022-1m)
- The WGS84 anchor is `[-0.12474687070, 51.4994]`. The GLB bakes positions in metres, X east, Y up and Z south, with Y=0 at 1.35 m Ordnance Datum Newlyn. The survey axes rotate 6.31° clockwise from east. The nominal grade is 4.4 m. The 1 m terrain raster gives 4.22 m inside the footprint of Victoria Tower, 2.53 m at Westminster Hall and 4.79 m at the riverside control point. [Environment Agency DTM](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/01b3ee39-da3f-47b6-83da-dc98e73a461f/lidar-composite-digital-terrain-model-dtm-1m)
- The mapped palace ring and courts come from OSM relation `1567699`. The plan uses the separate OSM outlines for the adjoining historic halls and Victoria Tower. A 0.65 m clearance from the generalised OpenFreeMap Thames polygon keeps the eastern edge on land in Relleu Obert. The adjustment is local to the river edge. [Palace relation](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/1567699) · [Victoria Tower outline](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/367642689) · [Westminster Hall outline](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/367642704)

## Validation

- The 60,804-byte GLB has 2,480 vertices and 5,028 triangles in one closed, consistently oriented solid. Vertical probes confirmed openings in all ten mapped courts and at the Elizabeth Tower’s anchor. The Victoria flagstaff and Central Tower reach 124.9 m and 95.8 m Newlyn. The Westminster Hall control reaches 30.36 m.
- In Relleu Obert 0.23.44, the OSM test at 1:2,300 placed the palace, abbey and Elizabeth Tower together and retained the independent New Palace Yard screen. The general palace volumes went from 358 to seven small fragments with zero plan overlap with the simplified palace footprint. Height limit and hidden-building modes passed. OpenFreeMap at 1:2,800 and 26° retained both adjacent catalogue models and replaced the mapped Central Tower. The OSM crop at 1:1,900 and −19°, shifted 100 m south, cut the north wing and produced a closed result. Each test placed the flagstaff tip at 124.9 m after accounting for vertical exaggeration.
- At 1:2,300 the geographic-scale mesh spans about 64 × 122 × 54 mm including its embedded foundation. The flagstaff tip is about 0.40 mm across at that scale. These are digital checks.
