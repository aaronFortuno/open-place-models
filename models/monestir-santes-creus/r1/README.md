# Reial Monestir de Santes Creus · r1

This model covers the abbey church, the main Gothic cloister, the rear cloister and their connecting buildings in Aiguamúrcia. It includes the church’s crenellated west front and large pointed window, its three-aisle roof profile, the octagonal crossing tower, the square Torre de les Hores and the hexagonal fountain pavilion in the main cloister. The rear wing includes the mapped mass of the royal palace and refectory. Independently mapped buildings around the core remain in the general building layer. [Monument guide](https://patrimoni.gencat.cat/ca/monuments/monuments/reial-monestir-de-santes-creus/reial-monestir-de-santes-creus) · [Architectural account of the palace and towers](https://www.enciclopedia.cat/lart-gotic-a-catalunya/el-monestir-de-santes-creus-segles-xiv-xv)

## Measurements and reconstruction

- **Plan.** The mapped footprint was checked against the ICGC orthophoto. Its exterior encloses about 7,101 m², with 1,151 m² in the two open courts. The main court is about 29 × 24 m and the rear court about 30 × 16 m. The fountain pavilion projects into the main court from the south gallery. The west entrance relief, arcades, roof junctions and small recesses in the palace wing are simplified for printing. [ICGC orthophoto](https://www.icgc.cat/es/Geoinformacion-y-mapas/Datos-y-productos/Imagen/Ortofoto-Territorial) · [Monument guide](https://patrimoni.gencat.cat/ca/monuments/monuments/reial-monestir-de-santes-creus/reial-monestir-de-santes-creus)
- **Roof levels.** The ICGC 1 m surface model places the central nave ridge around 335.6 m and the side aisles around 326–328 m. It gives about 330.6 m for the monks’ dormitory ridge and about 322–323 m for the cloister galleries. The crossing tower reaches 353.833 m and the Torre de les Hores 346.723 m. The historic survey gives a 71 m church length and 20.6 m central nave height. The 335.610 m ridge sample is about 20 m above the terrain beneath it. [Dimensions of Gothic buildings](https://www.enciclopedia.cat/art-de-catalunya/la-fabrica-gotica) · [ICGC surface model](https://catalegs.ide.cat/geonetwork/hub-litoral/api/records/model-superficies-lidar-catalunya-1m-2021-2023)
- **Terrain.** A 4 m grid sampled from the ICGC 50 cm bare-earth raster fits the underside to the site with a nominal 3 m embed. The 203 foundation vertices sit 2.63–3.56 m below the corresponding 50 cm cells. [ICGC terrain model and vertical reference](https://catalegs.ide.cat/geonetwork/geonetwork/api/records/model-elevacions-terreny-lidar-catalunya-50cm-2021-2023)

## Registration and reuse

The WGS84 longitude–latitude anchor is `[1.36398824, 41.34621999]` in the main cloister. GLB Y=0 is **309.776 m** orthometric elevation, referenced to EGM08D595. The GLB uses metres, with X east, Y up and Z south. It spans 140.04 × 107.55 m in plan and 44.06 m in height including the buried foundation.

The catalogue replaces OSM relation `3628735` version 9. It uses outer way `271832818` and the two courtyard ways `271835789` and `271835790`. The footprint has zero area overlap with the separately mapped OSM relations `7477818` and `7477819`. ## Validation

- The local catalogue and print loader accepted the 25,708-byte GLB. The mesh has one closed component, 1,035 vertices and 2,074 triangles, and a signed volume of 98,670.05 m³. The audit found zero boundary edges, non-manifold edges, winding conflicts, duplicate vertices or degenerate triangles. Each vertex has a single incident face ring. Vertical probes through both courts pass through the model unobstructed.
- In Relleu Obert, OpenStreetMap at 1:2,000 replaced relation `3628735` and kept both neighbouring relations. Vertical probes kept both courtyards open and the main peak was placed at 353.833 m. The height limit and hidden-building modes also passed. OpenFreeMap at 1:4,500 and a 26° rotation replaced the matching fragment `36287350`. A partial ICGC crop at 1:2,500 and a −19° rotation passed the solid audit. At full extent, ICGC footprint `icgc-1089210` groups the core with adjacent buildings. The model footprint covers 5,241 m² of that 12,645 m² feature, so the engine preserves the general volume and the two volumes overlap.
- The model is about 56.0 × 43.0 × 17.6 mm in plan and height at 1:2,500 and 28.0 × 21.5 × 8.8 mm at 1:5,000. The small façade and arcade relief will be finer at the smaller print scale. The 320 × 200 WebP thumbnail was checked visually. Slicing and physical printing are pending.
