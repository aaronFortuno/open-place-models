# Mont-Saint-Michel Abbey · r1

Le Mont-Saint-Michel, Normandy, France. The exterior model covers the abbey church, La Merveille and its cloister, the west terrace, and the attached abbatial lodgings and eastern entrance buildings. It includes the 1897 spire and a simplified figure of Saint Michael. The island's rock, village, outer ramparts and interiors are outside the model footprint. [Centre des monuments nationaux site plan](https://www.abbaye-mont-saint-michel.fr/decouvrir/s-orienter-dans-le-monument) · [Abbey church](https://www.abbaye-mont-saint-michel.fr/decouvrir/l-eglise-abbatiale)

## Measurements and reconstruction

- **Roofs and terraces.** A 0.5 m IGN MNS Correl GeoTIFF provides the outer roof and terrace heights. Its elevation values come from aerial image correlation. The approximate roof planes of the nave, transept, Gothic choir and refectory follow measured cross-sections, checked against the layout and aerial views. Their modelled ridges are about 101.6, 98.9, 111.0 and 92.2 m NGF-IGN69 respectively. The surface is smoothed and clipped to building outlines for the smaller bodies. The west pavement is at 79.83 m and the open cloister court floor is at 78.71 m, both from median surface samples. The cloister opening is about 19.3 × 13.0 m. Small windows, arcades and roof furniture are simplified into the main masses. [IGN MNS Correl](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/mns-correl) · [West terrace](https://www.abbaye-mont-saint-michel.fr/decouvrir/la-terrasse-de-l-ouest) · [Abbey and cloister plans](https://www.abbaye-mont-saint-michel.fr/decouvrir/s-orienter-dans-le-monument)
- **Tower and spire.** The IGN geodetic record 5035302-01 locates the centre of the ball above the belfry at 153.77 m NGF-IGN69. The photogrammetric surface peaks at 140.38 m within 0.4 m in plan of that surveyed axis. Lower tower and spire sections were estimated from the surrounding elevation samples. The narrow upper shaft above the sampled surface was reconstructed with the historic profile and fixed to the geodetic position. The nominal 157.27 m apex follows the operator's height of about 157 m and the scale of the sculpture. Its fused figure and wings are symbolic and thickened at the shaft. [IGN geodetic station](https://data.geopf.fr/annexes/geodesie/pt-rsgf-117719.pdf) · [Historical spire design, p. 201](https://www.iccrom.org/sites/default/files/2018-02/1987_metal_statuary_paris_40894_light.pdf) · [Operator's monument dimensions](https://www.montsaintmichel.gouv.fr/le-mont-et-sa-baie/un-bien-unesco)
- **Foundations.** The underside is a continuous surface fitted to the RGE ALTI terrain within the abbey plan. Its sampled elevations range from 39.45 to 78.37 m NGF-IGN69, with approximately 2–4 m of embedment before simplification. It accounts for the steep slope under the buildings. The irregular lower profile seen on the standalone mesh is the connection intended to sit inside the terrain model. [IGN RGE ALTI](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/rge-alti-r)

## Registration, source and reuse

The WGS84 anchor is `[-1.5114129, 48.6360346]`, at the plan position of the surveyed ball. GLB coordinates are metres with X east, Y up and Z south. The model's Y=0 is 39.447 m NGF-IGN69, specified separately as `anchorElevation` for Relleu Obert. This places the roofs at their documented elevations while preserving the hillside in the terrain source. The model spans about 116.62 × 87.69 m in plan and 117.82 m from its lowest foundation point to the top.

The surface and ground crops were obtained from IGN on 8 November 2026. The MNS Correl public catalogue reports an update dated 20 October 2026. The flight date of the surface crop is unspecified in its WMS delivery. `survey-layout.json` records the source requests and hashes, the surveyed axis, the sampled upper and lower envelopes and the fitted roof sections. ## Validation

- The GLB is 34,408 bytes with 1,378 vertices and 2,752 triangles. An independent audit found one watertight, consistently oriented solid with positive signed volume of 109,685.42 m³, a closed face ring at every vertex and zero coincident vertices or degenerate triangles.
- The raw Mapterhorn elevations at all 690 sampled underside vertices cover the model base. A 1:2,500 test with the initial client adjustment replaced 14 vector features and preserved the nearby Tour Gabriel. A 1:5,000 test exposed gaps with a coarser terrain mesh. The refined mesh sampling, rotated and cropped controls, and local gallery review are pending.
- At 1:2,500 the full solid spans about 47 × 35 × 47 mm, with a cloister opening about 7.7 × 5.2 mm. At 1:5,000 it spans about 23 × 18 × 24 mm, with an opening about 3.9 × 2.6 mm. The upper shaft narrows to about 0.29 mm at 1:5,000, so a 0.4 mm nozzle may omit portions of the finial. Slicing and physical printing remain to be checked.
