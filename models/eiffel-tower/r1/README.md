# Tour Eiffel · r1

Paris, France. The print mesh keeps the four feet, open base arches, platform decks, narrowing iron lattice and antenna from Newcandle’s print model. Its feet span a 125 m square and the tip reaches 330 m. The horizontal and vertical axes were scaled independently to those published dimensions. The platforms and individual foot widths follow the source mesh, with foot widths of approximately 27 m compared with 25 m in the operator’s figures. [Official dimensions](https://www.toureiffel.paris/fr/le-monument/chiffres-cle)

The origin is at ground level at `[2.2944961557127317, 48.85826211154724]` in WGS84 longitude and latitude. X points east, Y up and Z south. The base is rotated approximately 44.2° east of north, so its east–west and north–south spans are each 176.76 m. The contour of OSM way `5013364` was verified at version 187 on 7 November 2026 and matches the contour used for the Open Landmarks registration at version 181. The catalogue identifies `osm-way-5013364` for replacement in Relleu Obert. [OSM outline](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/5013364) · [Open Landmarks registration](https://open-landmarks.benmaps.fr/assets/eiffel-tower/4e011f6dced3b66b9599894f5e689977b06694c5d678a09872892d7f72e9a7d1/asset.json)

## Source and licence

Newcandle from Thingiverse created the original STL. Open Landmarks contributors registered it geographically. Open Place Models contributors welded the STL corners, scaled the mesh to the published height and width, baked the placement and orientation, and used Manifold to separate incident face rings and remove eight triangles. The original and adapted mesh are CC BY-SA 3.0, with the attribution also recorded in the GLB. The placement and footprint derive from OpenStreetMap under ODbL 1.0. [Original STL](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:EiffelTower_fixed.stl) · [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) · [OpenStreetMap attribution and ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)

After `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile`, run `node scripts/generate-eiffel.mjs` from the repository root to regenerate `print.glb`. The script checks the STL’s SHA-256 before processing it. Supply a local path to the same STL as the first argument to regenerate offline.

## Validation

- The GLB contains 60,956 indexed vertices and 147,596 triangles in one connected component. It is 1,618,228 bytes. The signed volume is 132,468 m³. The four feet and apex match the registered source exactly. The edge audit found zero boundary edges, edges shared by more than two faces or inconsistent face orientations. Each vertex index has one incident face ring. The canonical topology separates those rings at point contacts, with 62 additional indices sharing coordinates at 59 locations. These residual contacts remain relevant when checking the physical print in a slicer.
- Local Relleu Obert checks used the final GLB with Paris data. They covered full crops at 1:2,000 and 0°, 1:5,000 and 37°, 1:2,000 and 90° with original OSM data, and a partial crop at 8°. The model contacts the terrain, replaces the mapped tower while preserving neighbouring buildings, and respects the height limit and hidden-building controls.
- The 1:2,000 control exported as a 3MF with 166,708 triangles across four pieces. The standard 3MF control audit has a 100,000-triangle limit. An independent check parsed the archive and verified its assembly and materials, then used the indexed-solid audit on each of the four exported pieces. All four passed the edge, index and signed-volume checks.
- The full tower is 165 mm tall at 1:2,000 and 66 mm tall at 1:5,000 before adding the base. Sampled horizontal sections across fine members measured approximately 0.54–0.94 m across in the mesh. Those sections scale to 0.27–0.47 mm at 1:2,000 and 0.11–0.19 mm at 1:5,000. For a first physical trial, 1:1,500 gives a 220 mm tower and approximately 0.36–0.63 mm across those sections. Slicer behaviour, supports and physical printing are pending.
