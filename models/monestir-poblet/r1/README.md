# Monestir de Poblet · r1

This model covers the abbey church and the main cloister in Vimbodí i Poblet. The church includes the western entrance, pitched nave, side aisles, transept, octagonal crossing tower and projecting chapels around the apse. The main cloister has an open courtyard and an attached hexagonal fountain pavilion. The rest of the UNESCO site, including the Porta Reial, Palau de l’Abat, outer walls and New Sacristy, is outside this model’s footprint. [Abbey church](https://www.poblet.cat/ca/el-monument/esglesia-major/) · [Main cloister](https://www.poblet.cat/ca/el-monument/claustre-major/) · [UNESCO site](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/518/)

## Measurements and reconstruction

- **Plan.** The church and cloister outlines were checked against the ICGC 2025 25 cm orthophoto. The union contains approximately 2,409 m² of church and 882 m² of cloister roof and pavilion. The open court measures roughly 29 × 27 m before accounting for the pavilion, which projects from the north gallery. The mapped church extends about 86 m along its longitudinal axis, close to the published 85 m. [Architectural account](https://www.enciclopedia.cat/lart-gotic-a-catalunya/el-monestir-de-poblet-segles-xii-xiii) · [ICGC orthophoto](https://www.icgc.cat/es/Geoinformacion-y-mapas/Datos-y-productos/Imagen/Ortofoto-Territorial)
- **Roofs.** The ICGC 1 m surface model places the cloister gallery roofs around 498 m, the nave ridge around 514 m and the crossing crown at 534.383 m. The lower radial chapels follow the mapped east end. The secondary towers and arcade recesses are simplified for small prints. The present crossing tower roof was restored between 1978 and 1981. [Abbey account by the restoration architect](https://www.poblet.cat/media/upload/arxius/revista-poblet/revista-poblet-007.pdf) · [ICGC surface model](https://catalegs.ide.cat/geonetwork/hub-litoral/api/records/model-superficies-lidar-catalunya-1m-2021-2023)
- **New Sacristy.** The abbey describes a distinct 400 m² building outside the medieval enclosure with a 43 m lantern. The surface model samples its crown at 537.449 m, about 44.6 m above the local bare-earth sample. This building is mapped separately in OSM and stays in the general building layer. [Abbey architectural notes](https://www.poblet.cat/media/upload/arxius/2-1-1-apunts-d-art-i-historia-de-poblet.pdf) · [New Sacristy](https://www.poblet.cat/ca/el-monument/esglesia-major/sagristia-nova/) · [OSM way 174173351](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/174173351)
- **Terrain.** The foundation follows a 4 m grid sampled from the ICGC 50 cm bare-earth raster with a nominal 3 m embed. The 182 underside vertices sit 2.19–3.72 m below the corresponding source raster cells. The foundation carries the model into the terrain and is visible when the GLB is viewed on its own. [ICGC terrain model and vertical reference](https://catalegs.ide.cat/geonetwork/geonetwork/api/records/model-elevacions-terreny-lidar-catalunya-50cm-2021-2023)

## Registration and reuse

The plan anchor is `[1.08318238, 41.38069575]` in WGS84 longitude–latitude order. GLB Y=0 is **488.389 m** orthometric elevation, referenced to EGM08D595. The GLB uses metres, with X east, Y up and Z south. Its extent is 86.21 × 73.08 m in plan and 45.99 m high including the embedded foundation.

The mapped footprint is the union of church way `174173363` version 9 and main cloister relation `2325532` version 4. It shares edges with the adjacent dependencies in relation `2325531` and has zero area overlap with that relation and New Sacristy way `174173351`. The two replaced source features are recorded explicitly. [OSM church](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/174173363) · [OSM main cloister](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/2325532) · [OSM adjacent dependencies](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/2325531)

## Validation

- The local gallery and print loader accepted the 25,828-byte GLB. The mesh has one closed component, 1,041 vertices and 2,082 triangles, and a signed volume of 58,361.22 m³. The audit found zero boundary edges, non-manifold edges, winding conflicts, duplicate vertices or degenerate triangles. Each vertex has a single incident face ring.
- The model measures about 34.5 × 29.2 × 18.4 mm in plan and height at 1:2,500, and 17.2 × 14.6 × 9.2 mm at 1:5,000. The 2.94 m-wide arcade recesses may be faint at 1:5,000, when their depth is about 0.23 mm. The 320 × 200 WebP thumbnail was checked visually. Physical printing and slicing are pending.
- In Relleu Obert, OpenStreetMap at 1:2,500 replaced the church and main cloister while preserving the adjacent relation. OpenFreeMap at 1:5,000 replaced the two matching fragments and kept the other 18 in the surrounding group. A partial crop with ICGC passed the mesh audit. At full extent the ICGC source groups the church with adjacent buildings in one footprint, which Relleu Obert preserves, so that source produces overlapping volumes at this site.
