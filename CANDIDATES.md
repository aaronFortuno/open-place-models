# Candidate queue

Research snapshot · 7 November 2026

The order balances a recognisable silhouette, evidence for its dimensions, and a viable route to a printable asset. Each revision should start with one place. Follow the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) for georeferencing, licence attribution and validation. The coordinates below are WGS84 search centres in longitude–latitude order. Verify the final footprint, base anchor and orientation against the source before publishing. The scales are proposed review scales.

## First modelling passes

### 1. Tour Eiffel · Paris, France · `eiffel-tower`

- **Search and footprint.** `[2.29450, 48.85826]`. The georeferenced reference covers approximately `[2.29331, 48.85747, 2.29569, 48.85905]`. The four feet form a rotated square with a 125 m side. Compare the mapped outline with the actual foot contacts and record whether the catalogue footprint describes the entire structure or its four ground contacts. The source's OpenStreetMap reference is way 5013364, version 181, dated 12 May 2026. Open Landmarks' Protomaps identifiers belong to its own tileset and need independent verification for a replacement in Relleu Obert. [Source metadata and footprint](https://open-landmarks.benmaps.fr/assets/eiffel-tower/4e011f6dced3b66b9599894f5e689977b06694c5d678a09872892d7f72e9a7d1/asset.json) · [OSM feature](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/5013364)
- **Shape and scale.** The operator reports 330 m overall and platforms at 57, 115 and 276 m. The first revision retains four splayed legs, open arches and the taper towards the antenna. Local controls use 1:2,000 and 1:5,000, where the overall height is 165 and 66 mm. [Operator dimensions](https://www.toureiffel.paris/fr/le-monument/chiffres-cle) · [Revision 1](models/eiffel-tower/r1/README.md)
- **Source route used.** Revision 1 adapts the closed STL by Newcandle from Wikimedia Commons under CC BY-SA 3.0. Open Landmarks supplies registration and visual reference. Its low GLB has open and non-manifold edges. OSM-derived spatial data retain ODbL 1.0. [Original STL and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:EiffelTower_fixed.stl) · [Open Landmarks audit](https://open-landmarks.benmaps.fr/assets/eiffel-tower/4e011f6dced3b66b9599894f5e689977b06694c5d678a09872892d7f72e9a7d1/validation.json)

### 2. Catedral de Barcelona · Barcelona, Spain · `barcelona-cathedral`

- **Search and footprint.** `[2.17618, 41.38396]`. The cathedral gives an external envelope of 93 × 40 m. Identify the nave, apse, side chapels and cloister separately. Agree whether the first revision covers the church alone or includes the adjoining cloister, and record the mapped outline of the chosen scope. [Cathedral architecture and dimensions](https://catedralbcn.org/en/the-cathedral/building/)
- **Shape and scale.** Use the 28 m nave, 54 m bell towers and 70 m cimbori as vertical controls. Retain the façade silhouette, main spire, stepped roofs and apse. At 1:1,000 and 1:2,500 the main spire is about 70 and 28 mm high. [Cathedral architecture and dimensions](https://catedralbcn.org/en/the-cathedral/building/)
- **Source route.** The municipal CAD 3D product 146 lists the Barri Gòtic distribution under CC BY 4.0. Its direct 10.1 MB ZIP contains Barri_02_MTM_DWG.dwg, in ETRS89 / UTM 31N (EPSG:25831). A lightweight header check confirmed the archive and DWG filename. Open the drawing and check whether it resolves the roofs, bell towers and cimbori. If those volumes are missing, use the official dimensions and a verified footprint for an original reconstruction. [Product and licence](https://portaldades.ajuntament.barcelona.cat/mapes/146) · [Municipal download feed](https://w20.bcn.cat/CartoBCN/atom.ashx?file=146) · [Barri Gòtic ZIP](https://w20.bcn.cat/CartoBCN/getFile.ashx?prod=146.02.47)

### 3. Torre pendente di Pisa · Pisa, Italy · `leaning-tower-pisa`

- **Search and footprint.** `[10.39660, 43.72302]`. Start with the campanile only. The custodian reports a 15 m outside diameter and a height of 58.36 m. Survey the lean direction and base elevation separately. Document the definition of the tilt and distinguish it from the offset of the uppermost stage. The stabilisation study includes the geometry and interventions needed to check this. [Custodian specifications](https://www.opapisa.it/visita/torre-pendente/) · [Stabilisation study](https://geocasehistoriesjournal.org/pub/article/download/IJGCH_1_3_2/86?paper=true)
- **Shape and scale.** Preserve the lean, stacked circular loggias and smaller bell chamber. Compare 1:750 and 1:1,500, giving a footprint diameter of about 20 and 10 mm. Test which open galleries survive and use relief bands where necessary. [Custodian description and views](https://www.opapisa.it/visita/torre-pendente/)
- **Source route.** Build an original parametric version from the published survey dimensions and a separately sourced anchor. The CyArk and University of Ferrara terrestrial scan on Open Heritage 3D is CC BY-NC-SA. Keep any version derived from that scan on hold for permission that allows the catalogue's intended redistribution and reuse. [Scan record and licence](https://openheritage3d.org/project.php?id=dbg6-x966)

### 4. Basílica de la Sagrada Família · Barcelona, Spain · `sagrada-familia`

- **Search and footprint.** `[2.17436, 41.40363]`. The scope is the temple and its built façades, towers and apse. Resolve attached volumes against the available footprint and keep surrounding buildings separate. Date the revision and reconcile the survey with the foundation's construction record. The foundation records the final piece of the central cross on 20 February 2026. [Foundation announcement](https://sagradafamilia.org/es/-/una-creu-que-arriba-al-cel-de-barcelona) · [Architectural description](https://sagradafamilia.org/documents/20142/1205286/SF_Booklet_04_20240405_digital_AF.pdf/29d08418-9034-2d14-58fb-b6a5d0c3ff09)
- **Shape and scale.** Preserve the Latin cross, the Nativity and Passion towers, and the group of six central towers. The foundation gives 172.5 m for the central tower and 135 m for the Evangelists. Compare 1:1,500 and 1:3,000, where the central tower is 115 and 57.5 mm. Check the cross and pinnacles against print thickness, and account for the built state of the Glory façade at the revision date. [Foundation tower specifications](https://sagradafamilia.org/documents/20142/1693659/SF_Booklet_10_20260227_digital_AF.pdf/b2061581-3ab8-b179-44b6-7825004f4162)
- **Source route.** Inspect the municipal Sagrada Família DWG for the temple's dated geometry and exact reuse terms. The ICGC photogrammetric viewer comes from a December 2014 flight and provides historical reference. Confirm a downloadable file and explicit redistribution terms before using its geometry. The current structure requires recent evidence for elements built after that flight. [Municipal CAD 3D downloads](https://portaldades.ajuntament.barcelona.cat/mapes/146) · [ICGC photogrammetric prototype](https://betaportal.icgc.cat/model-3d-de-la-sagrada-familia-en-alta-resolucio/)

### 5. Basilica di San Marco · Venice, Italy · `st-marks-basilica`

- **Search and footprint.** `[12.33967, 45.43457]`. Include the basilica's exterior and roof. Treat the nearby freestanding campanile and adjoining Palazzo Ducale as separate structures. Obtain plan dimensions, dome heights and a verified footprint before modelling. [Basilica façade documentation](https://www.basilicasanmarco.it/en/sculpture/the-decoration-of-the-facades/) · [UNESCO site context](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/394/)
- **Shape and scale.** Preserve the five domes, cross-shaped body and rhythm of the façade arches. Compare 1:1,000 and 1:2,500 with views from the piazza and from above. Check which details distinguish the basilica at the smaller scale. [San Marco 3D survey project](https://www.unescolab.mantova.polimi.it/elementor-796/)
- **Source route.** The Politecnico di Milano and Procuratoria document an HBIM survey designed for 1:50 use. Ask for an exterior subset and explicit adaptation and redistribution terms, or source published dimensioned elevations for an original reconstruction. The project page describes the survey and gives a contact for access. [San Marco 3D project and contacts](https://www.unescolab.mantova.polimi.it/elementor-796/)
