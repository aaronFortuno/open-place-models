# Candidate queue

Research snapshot · 7 November 2026

The order balances a recognisable silhouette, evidence for its dimensions, and a viable route to a printable asset. Each revision should start with one place. Follow the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) for georeferencing, licence attribution and validation. The coordinates below are WGS84 search centres in longitude–latitude order. Verify the final footprint, base anchor and orientation against the source before publishing. The scales are proposed review scales.

## First modelling passes

### 1. Tour Eiffel · Paris, France · `eiffel-tower`

- **Search and footprint.** `[2.29450, 48.85826]`. The georeferenced reference covers approximately `[2.29331, 48.85747, 2.29569, 48.85905]`. The four feet form a rotated square with a 125 m side. Compare the mapped outline with the actual foot contacts and record whether the catalogue footprint describes the entire structure or its four ground contacts. The source's OpenStreetMap reference is way 5013364, version 181, dated 12 May 2026. Open Landmarks' Protomaps identifiers belong to its own tileset and need independent verification for a replacement in Relleu Obert. [Source metadata and footprint](https://open-landmarks.benmaps.fr/assets/eiffel-tower/4e011f6dced3b66b9599894f5e689977b06694c5d678a09872892d7f72e9a7d1/asset.json) · [OSM feature](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/5013364)
- **Shape and scale.** The operator reports 330 m overall and platforms at 57, 115 and 276 m. The lightweight default retains four splayed legs, open arches and the taper towards the antenna. The detailed variant retains the lattice and is suggested at 1:1,500 or closer. Local controls use 1:2,000 and 1:5,000, where the overall height is 165 and 66 mm. [Operator dimensions](https://www.toureiffel.paris/fr/le-monument/chiffres-cle) · [Lightweight revision](models/eiffel-tower/r2/README.md) · [Detailed revision](models/eiffel-tower/r1/README.md)
- **Source route used.** Revision 1 adapts the closed STL by Newcandle from Wikimedia Commons under CC BY-SA 3.0. Open Landmarks supplies registration and visual reference. Its low GLB has open and non-manifold edges. OSM-derived spatial data retain ODbL 1.0. [Original STL and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:EiffelTower_fixed.stl) · [Open Landmarks audit](https://open-landmarks.benmaps.fr/assets/eiffel-tower/4e011f6dced3b66b9599894f5e689977b06694c5d678a09872892d7f72e9a7d1/validation.json)

### 2. Catedral de Barcelona · Barcelona, Spain · `barcelona-cathedral`

- **Search and footprint.** `[2.17618, 41.38396]`. The cathedral gives a church envelope of 93 × 40 m. The model covers the church and the adjoining cloister with its open courtyard. [Cathedral architecture and dimensions](https://catedralbcn.org/en/the-cathedral/building/) · [Revision 2](models/barcelona-cathedral/r2/README.md) · [Revision 1](models/barcelona-cathedral/r1/README.md)
- **Shape and scale.** Revision 2 uses measured roof levels and bell tower terraces, simplified buttresses, and the 78 m central cross from the LiDAR sections. The controls use 1:1,000 and 1:2,500. [Revision 2 measurements and validation](models/barcelona-cathedral/r2/README.md)
- **Source route used.** The municipal CAD 3D product 146 supplies Barri_02_MTM_DWG.dwg in ETRS89 / UTM 31N under CC BY 4.0. The drawing gives footprints and flat roof heights. Revision 2 retains the CAD plan and adds ICGC LiDAR sections for the roof levels and tower profiles. Eduardo Henrique’s model provides an exterior visual reference. [LiDAR Territorial](https://www.icgc.cat/ca/Geoinformacio-i-mapes/Dades-i-productes/Elevacions/Elevacions-territorial/LiDAR-Territorial) · [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cathedral-of-bcnsanta-creu-and-santa-eulalia-4395e17feb95400c891597121a59513f) [Product and licence](https://portaldades.ajuntament.barcelona.cat/mapes/146) · [Barri Gòtic ZIP](https://w20.bcn.cat/CartoBCN/getFile.ashx?prod=146.02.47)

### 3. Basílica de la Sagrada Família · Barcelona, Spain · `sagrada-familia`

- **Search and footprint.** `[2.17436, 41.40363]`. The scope is the temple and its built façades, towers and apse. Resolve attached volumes against the available footprint and keep surrounding buildings separate. Date the revision and reconcile the survey with the foundation's construction record. The foundation records the final piece of the central cross on 20 February 2026. [Foundation announcement](https://sagradafamilia.org/es/-/una-creu-que-arriba-al-cel-de-barcelona) · [Architectural description](https://sagradafamilia.org/documents/20142/1205286/SF_Booklet_04_20240405_digital_AF.pdf/29d08418-9034-2d14-58fb-b6a5d0c3ff09)
- **Shape and scale.** Preserve the Latin cross, the Nativity and Passion towers, and the group of six central towers. The foundation gives 172.5 m for the central tower and 135 m for the Evangelists. Compare 1:1,500 and 1:3,000, where the central tower is 115 and 57.5 mm. Check the cross and pinnacles against print thickness, and account for the built state of the Glory façade at the revision date. [Foundation tower specifications](https://sagradafamilia.org/documents/20142/1693659/SF_Booklet_10_20260227_digital_AF.pdf/b2061581-3ab8-b179-44b6-7825004f4162)
- **Source route used.** Revision 1 combines the municipal CAD volumes, tower sections measured from the ICGC December 2014 aerial survey, and the Foundation’s dimensions for the six central towers completed in 2026. See the revision for source attribution, the geometry simplified for printing and the local validation results. [Revision 1](models/sagrada-familia/r1/README.md)

### 4. Torre pendente di Pisa · Pisa, Italy · `leaning-tower-pisa`

- **Search and footprint.** The campanile anchor is `[10.39663235, 43.7230066]`, with the ground outline from OpenStreetMap relation 12982355. The geometry reaches 56.70 m above the local ground, following the published survey height range. The custodian reports 58.36 m without specifying the datum, close to the 58.4 m measured from the foundation in the engineering literature. [Custodian specifications](https://www.opapisa.it/visita/torre-pendente/) · [Published radar survey](https://academic.oup.com/jge/article/11/5/055004/5110272) · [Revision 1](models/leaning-tower-pisa/r1/README.md)
- **Shape and scale.** The model uses the measured curve of the leaning axis, six loggias with arcades in relief, and the smaller open bell chamber. The local controls use 1:750 and 1:1,500. [2001 survey, figures 3–7](https://www.isprs.org/proceedings/xxxiv/5-W12/proceedings/04.pdf)
- **Source route used.** The original parametric reconstruction combines published survey dimensions, the post-stabilisation monitoring results and the OSM registration. The 2021 Tuscany surface model provides an independent check of the southward lean. [Stabilisation study](https://geocasehistoriesjournal.org/issues/volume-1-3/leaning-tower-of-pisa-behaviour-after-stabilization-operations) · [Regional survey catalogue](https://www502.regione.toscana.it/geoscopio/servizi/wms/CARTOTECA.htm)

### 5. Basilica di San Marco · Venice, Italy · `st-marks-basilica`

- **Search and footprint.** The basilica uses the outline of OpenStreetMap way 138800932, with its anchor at the measured centre of the central dome. The Campanile and Palazzo Ducale remain separate. [OSM footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/138800932) · [Revision 1](models/st-marks-basilica/r1/README.md)
- **Shape and scale.** Preserve the five distinct dome profiles and the five façade bays. The surface survey measures the main roof, and published dimensions define the higher finials. Local controls use 1:1,000 and 1:2,500. [Basilica façade documentation](https://www.basilicasanmarco.it/en/sculpture/the-decoration-of-the-facades/)
- **Source route used.** The 2014 aerial surface model of the Città Metropolitana di Venezia provides elevations at a 0.25 m grid interval under CC BY 4.0. The model also uses historical façade plates and published lightning protection elevations. The Politecnico di Milano and Procuratoria HBIM study documents a separate survey. [Metropolitan survey and licence](https://geodati.gov.it/geoportale/visualizzazione-metadati/scheda-metadati?metadataid=p_ve:000045:20241031:114741) · [Ongania plates](https://ongania-sanmarco.it/biblioteca_digitale/1-Portafoglio-1) · [HBIM project](https://www.unescolab.mantova.polimi.it/elementor-796/)
